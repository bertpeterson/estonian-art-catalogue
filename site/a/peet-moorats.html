Peet Moorats

1881–1908 · Estonian graphic artist (1880-1908)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Viljandi Muuseum

trained at Imperial Academy of Arts · trained at Saint Petersburg Stieglitz State Academy of Art and Design

He practised tonal woodcut, drawing and painting. He became known for the monument to J. Kapp and the woodcut depicting J. Köler's sarcophagus. His woodcut-technique caricatures and decorations appeared in the years 1899-1908 in the Viljandi newspaper "Rahvaleht", in the supplement "Ulak" of the newspaper "Kodumaa" (title decoration) and in the supplement of "Postimees". Also in the holiday albums published by J. Ploompuu, and in the Russian picture magazine "Нива". He painted portraits of cultural figures and society activists of Suure-Jaani and its surroundings. He designed the flag of the Suure-Jaani Fire Brigade Society and the stage curtain of the fire station. Of his reproductive graphic works, the most well-known and technically most successful is the woodcut of I. Repin's painted portrait of L. Tolstoy.
