Boris Lukats

1907–1996 · Estonian printmaker (1907-1996)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Pallas

He studied at the Tallinn Peetri Secondary School, in 1925-1928 (with interruptions) at the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics of the University of Tartu, and in 1928-1936 at the "Pallas" art school in Tartu. He was mobilised into the Red Army in 1941, and stayed in the rear of the USSR in Yaroslavl and Moscow. In 1946-1951 he was a lecturer at the Tallinn State Institute of Applied Art, and in 1947-1950 chairman of the Artists' Union of the ESSR. In 1952-1967 he worked in Viljandi in various positions (director of a children's home, etc.) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
