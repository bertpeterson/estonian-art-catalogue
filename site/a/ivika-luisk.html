Ivika Luisk

1969–
Haus Galerii; NOBA

Ivika Luisk (b.1969) is a painter and puppet-film artist who lives and works in Tallinn. She completed her bachelor's studies in painting and monumental painting at the Estonian Academy of Arts in 1995 and her master's studies in 1998. Luisk is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Painters' Association. The artist has so far had 15 solo exhibitions, has participated in several group exhibitions and has worked as an artist on 15 puppet films. In her work, an important place is occupied by the human being and nature, their relationship and mutual influence. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
