Esti Kittus

1948–
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

Esti Kittus (born 7 April 1948 in Tartu) is an Estonian theatre artist and art teacher. She studied in 1963–1966 at the Tartu Art School and graduated in 1966 from the Tartu Evening Secondary School and in 1972 in the speciality of theatre artist-pedagogue from the Estonian SSR State Art Institute (supervisor Mari-Liis Küla). Kittus worked from 1972 to 2003 as an artist at the Vanemuine Theatre. She has also designed the Tartu song and dance festivals. In 2004 she founded the Puka Art School and worked there until 2021. She is a member of the Estonian Theatre Union (1974), the Estonian Association of Stage Designers (1993; honorary member) and the Estonian Artists' Association (1995). She also belongs to the Tartu Artists' Union. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
