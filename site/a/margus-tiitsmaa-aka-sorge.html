Margus Tiitsmaa aka Sorge

1963–
NOBA

Margus Tiitsmaa (b. 1963), also known as Sorge, has had a long and colourful artistic path. During the Soviet era he studied in the art department of the pedagogical institute, worked as a window designer for a department store, and secretly performed performances in the Saue grove. He was not accepted to ERKI, because Sorge's mindset in every sense went beyond the standards and did not fit within the framework of traditional art education.
