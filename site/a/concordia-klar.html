Concordia Klar

1938–2004 · Estonian printmaker (1938-2004)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Narva Muuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Russia, in 1942 the family moved to Estonia, to Rakvere. Studied at the Tartu Art School in 1952-1957 and graphic art at ERKI (EKA) in 1957-1963, was a lecturer at TPÜ (TLÜ) in 1966-1994. Received the Grand Prix at the III Tallinn Graphic Art Triennial. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
