Arkadio Laigo

1901–1944 · Estonian painter and printmaker (1901-1944)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haapsalu ja Läänemaa Muuseumid; Haus Galerii

trained at Pallas

He was born in Tallinn as the son of a Ukrainian coppersmith, studied painting at "Pallas" in Tartu in 1922–27. In 1929, 1936–37 and 1939 he was on study trips to Paris. In 1939 he married Anita Obst (Oeste). In 1927–37 he was a free artist in Võru, in 1937–41 a teacher of graphic art at "Pallas". In 1937 he received an honourable diploma at the Paris World Exhibition, and in 1939 and 1940 the Estonian Book Fund prize. In 1940 he joined the reorganisers of art life in Tartu. He was arrested by the Germans on 26.05.1943. The portfolio "Põlevkivitööstus" ["Oil Shale Industry"], the signature of which includes bars, was partly completed in prison. He was presumably executed on 23.07.1944 near Tallinn. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
