Sasha Tishkov

1989–
NOBA

Sasha Tishkov (1989) is a visual artist and carpenter whose work focuses on the dynamics between peripheries and centres. His sculptural objects and installations explore environmental and human phenomena. In his work he uses natural materials and traditional craft techniques, such as woodworking and weaving – his interest in craft began in childhood, which he spent in Matsalu National Park. Tishkov obtained a master's degree at Central Saint Martins in London (2019) and works both in Estonia and internationally. He is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association.
