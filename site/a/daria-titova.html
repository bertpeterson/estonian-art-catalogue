Daria Titova


NOBA

Daria Titova is a Ukrainian artist who began her studies at the Kharkiv Art School and is currently continuing them at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Her work focuses on themes of colonialism and national identity, weaving together personal memories and sensitive experiences. Embroidery serves as a poetic tool for expressing intimate stories and collective memory. In her practice, print, text, video, embroidery, and moving image converge, as she explores the experience of presence and embodiment through materiality.
