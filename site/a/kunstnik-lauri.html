Kunstnik LAURi


NOBA

In his work LAURi deals with the pain points of the human soul, manipulating and dislocating reality. LAURi has taken part in group exhibitions, most recently at the exhibition "Kui ma elust peaks ärkama" held in Haapsalu, where his work "100" received media attention.
