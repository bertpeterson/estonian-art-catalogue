Eve Ermann

1972–
Artrovert; NOBA

Eve Ermann (b. 1972) graduated from the EKA Department of Glass Art (1999) with a BA, followed by the EKA teacher training programme (2001). She has worked as a wall painting conservator-restorer. She completed the EKA Open Academy continuing education programme in Architectural Conservation and Restoration (2014). From 2016 she has been a member of the Estonian Watercolour Society. She has taken part in joint exhibitions, the pan-European ECWS (European Confederation of Watercolour Societies) exhibitions in Avignon, Salamanca, Kraków, Ulm, Bilbao. Also international watercolour exhibitions in Italy, in Fabriano, and in Finland, in Helsinki. She has had five solo exhibitions. She has taken part in percent-for-art competitions, among them in 2021 one of seven winning works at the art competition of the Estonian Embassy in Washington, and in 2023 third place at the art competition for the building of the Estonian Knighthood. The nature and aesthetics of her paintings have been influenced by her work on historical sites. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
