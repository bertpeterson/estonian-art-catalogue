Naima Neidre

1943– · Estonian artist (born 1943)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Naima Neidre (1943) is a graphic artist, illustrator and watercolourist who has long worked at a high level, whose works, marked by an extremely sensitive visual style, weave together stories of nature, the sea and people. Neidre graduated from ERKI in graphic art (1971) and has participated in numerous exhibitions both in Estonia and internationally. Her works can be found in many (private) collections, and she has received several honours, including the Kristjan Raud Prize (2004). Neidre is a member of the Estonian Association of Free Graphic Artists and an honorary member of the Estonian Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
