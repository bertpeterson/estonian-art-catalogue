Grisli Soppe-Kahar

1990–
NOBA

Grisli Soppe-Kahar (b. 1990) is an artist who lives and works in Tallinn. She graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts' department of fine arts in painting with a BA (2014) and MA (2016). Her first solo exhibition took place in 2015 at Draakoni Gallery; in 2017 she had a large-scale exhibition together with Danel Kahar in the large hall of the Tartu Art House. In the summer of 2018 she was in residence together with Danel Kahar in Italy (Mazzano Romano). Her most recent solo exhibition took place in 2021 at the Tallinn Portrait Gallery ("Someone Else").
