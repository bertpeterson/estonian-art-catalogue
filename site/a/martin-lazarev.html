Martin Lazarev

1977–
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Martin Lazarev (b. 1977 in Tartu) has studied furniture design, wood restoration and classical drawing at the Tartu Art College. The artist has studied photographic art within the framework of courses at the Tartu Art College, but has mainly acquired his knowledge on his own, through practice. His working life has brought Lazarev together with several different design and advertising agencies. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
