Juhan Püttsepp

1898–1975 · Estonian painter (1898–1975)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

trained at Pallas

Worked as a teacher in Põltsamaa and in vocational and secondary schools in Tartu, was dean of Pallas in 1943–1944 and of the Tartu State Art Institute in 1944–1951, worked at ERKI in 1951–1957 and from 1957 took up work at the University of Tartu.
