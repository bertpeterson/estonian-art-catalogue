Anna-Liisa Sääsk


NOBA; Ruki galerii

Anna-Liisa Sääsk is a young artist and designer whose work focuses on graphics, performance and various fields of visual media. Her earlier recognitions include the Ilon Wikland Young Artist Award and recognition for organising the contemporary art festival "Kunstikompott" for young people. From Kunstikompott and a small group at the art school arose the art group TEMA, which has participated in several festivals with its performances and installations, such as the Diverse Universe performance festival in Pärnu, Tartu Valgus, Viljandi Tuled and the Made in Estonia marathon 2024.
