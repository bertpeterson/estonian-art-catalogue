Alexander Webber


NOBA

Alexander Webber is a British born artist, studying at The Royal Academy of Art in The Netherlands. Webber is currently coming to the end of his Erasmus exchange in the sculpture and Installation department at EKA. He utilises a wide range of media to investigate the shifting symbolic and cultural capital that has embedded itself into society. Often drawing on personal experiences of design and the fashion industry, eurocentricity is digested through his cynical and darkly humorous perspective.
