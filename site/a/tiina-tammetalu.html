Tiina Tammetalu

1961– · Estonian painter (born 1961)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Tiina Tammetalu (1961) prefers to define herself as an artist of fluid identity, transcending media and not to be pigeonholed, who realises herself simultaneously in several different creative fields: painting, graphic design, illustration, and, among other things, also curates exhibitions of living Estonian painting classics. Tammetalu graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute in painting (1989) and furthered her education in the interdisciplinary arts master's programme at the Estonian Academy of Arts (2003–2006). She has participated in numerous exhibitions both in Estonia and elsewhere, and her works belong to several collections. She is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association.
