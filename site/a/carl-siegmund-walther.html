Carl Siegmund Walther

1783–1866
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Studied at the Dresden Academy of Art. Came to Estonia in 1809 at the invitation of the writer August von Kotzebue to be a drawing teacher for his children. Later worked as a drawing teacher in Hageri at the church school founded by pastor F. Ignatius, and from 1815 at the Tallinn Cathedral School. Retired in 1850, lived at Kaiu Manor, which belonged to his daughter. He mostly painted altarpieces (in the churches of Tallinn and Kuressaare, in Ambla Church, etc.), as well as portraits; drawings depicting the ruins of St Olaf's Church after the fire of 1820 originate from him, after which lithographic sheets were also made. He founded the first lithography workshop in Tallinn. He also carried out commissions for portraits of Russian tsars and ceremonial portraits.
