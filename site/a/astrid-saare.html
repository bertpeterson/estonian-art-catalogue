Astrid Saare

1929–2016 · Estonian sculptor (1929-2016)
Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

A Tartu painter and pedagogue. In 1949 he/she began studying painting at the Tartu State Art Institute, in 1955 graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute. From 1957-1992 he/she worked as a drawing teacher at the Tartu Art School, and thereafter, until retirement, also as a painting teacher. He/she began exhibiting at exhibitions from 1956.
