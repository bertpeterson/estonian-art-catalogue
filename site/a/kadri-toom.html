Kadri Toom

1984–
Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Ruki galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

Kadri Toom (b. 1984) graduated from the graphic arts department of Eesti Kunstiakadeemia with a master's degree (2009), from the painting department of Tartu Kõrgem Kunstikool (2006), and has also continued her studies at Saimaa Rakenduskõrgkool (2005).
