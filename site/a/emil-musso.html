Emil Musso

1857–1936 · Estonian painter and local politician (1857-1936)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Imperial University of Dorpat

Tartu town councillor and painter. Graduated from the gymnasium in Tartu in 1876, studied oil and watercolour painting in his home town under Woldemar Friedrich Krüger and Julie Wilhelmine Hagen-Schwarz. Continued his art studies in Vienna under William Unger and in Berlin under Gustav Richter. Ran a brewery in Tartu from 1880 to 1897. Became a citizen of Tartu in 1881 and was a town councillor until 1897. Was a Tartu town councillor in 1888–1889. Worked as a bank official in Pskov, Łódź, Šiauliai and Riga from 1898 to 1920. Painted landscapes in oil and watercolour technique. Exhibited in Tartu, Riga, St. Petersburg and Moscow. - Wikipedia. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
