Arseni Mölder

1919–2009 · Estonian artist (1919-2009)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

His oeuvre is divided into several different periods. The sculptures made in the 1950s belong to the finest examples of the realistic style of the time (a joint work with his wife Signe Mölder is the now-relocated monument to V.I. Lenin in Jõhvi (together with Enn Roos)). Arseni Mölder's most outstanding monumental work remains the monument to N. Pirogov in Tartu, executed in a classicist order (1952, together with Juhan Raudsepp), the bronze relief "The Battle of Assamalla" of the F.R. Kreutzwald monument (1957), and several memorial plaques executed in precious materials, among other works. His finest free-standing sculptures ("The Moulder" (1950, bronze 1964), "Relay Runners" (bronze, 1956)). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
