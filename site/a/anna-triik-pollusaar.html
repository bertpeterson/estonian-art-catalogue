Anna Triik-Põllusaar

1902–1998 · Estonian artist (1901-1998)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Pallas

Studied 1919–1926 at Pallas, graduated in 1926 as a painter, a student of Nikolai Triik. Already in 1923 received the qualification of drawing teacher. 1932–1935 studied sculpture at Pallas, worked with watercolour. After World War II moved to Australia in 1949, where she married Erik Kompus. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
