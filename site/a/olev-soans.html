Olev Soans

1925–1995 · Estonian painter (1925-1995)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Printmaker. Was one of the renewers of Estonian graphic art in the 1960s (introduced electrographic screen printing). Sought documentary quality in his work. Alongside free graphic art he cultivated book graphics with novel compositional approaches (P. Rummo's "Proloog ja probleemid" 1961, H. Jürisson's "Puu silmapiiril" 1966). From 1975 he designed a series of infographic maps, including a cultural-historical atlas of Estonia (writers' birthplaces, architectural monuments etc. 1995). Works: Praam (1959), Küpresside armastuslaul (1964), Päikeselill (1966), Kaks korda kahekesi (1968), infographic series Eesti purjesõidu ajalugu (1981) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
