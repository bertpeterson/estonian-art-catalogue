Tiina Ojaste

1961–
NOBA

Tiina Ojaste (b. 1961 in Pärnu) began her art studies at the age of eight. In 1981 she graduated from the Tartu Art College in leatherworking. After her studies she began working as an artist at the ELVI Special Design Bureau. A few years later she became an entrepreneur, and for about twenty years worked as a leather artist and designer in companies she had founded herself.
