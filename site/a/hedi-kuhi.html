Hedi Kuhi


Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Hedi Kuhi (b 1998) is a painter who lives and works both in Tallinn and in Valga. In 2019 she graduated from the Tartu Art School in the speciality of decorator, and in 2026 from the Pallas University of Applied Sciences in the speciality of painting. In 2023 Hedi was awarded the Hotell Pallas scholarship, as a result of which the solo exhibition "Jälle siin" ("Here Again") was created at the Tartu Art House. In 2025 she was awarded the Eduard Wiiralt scholarship and the Pallas field scholarship in the arts. In the autumn of the same year Hedi took part, together with Danel Ülper, in the duo exhibition "Ega sa siin Valgas Marki ei tea? Lugu piirilinnast" ("You Wouldn't Happen to Know Mark Here in Valga? A Story of a Border Town") at Hobusepea Gallery. She has also taken part in several group exhibitions and auctions. In her work Hedi focuses on the perception of the surrounding environment. She is interested in the balance between wildness and order, both in urban space and in a person's inner world. Figures, nature motifs and cityscapes are often layered together in her paintings. In creating her works she uses the technique of double exposure, as a result of which the depicted views feel as though suspended between the dreamlike and the real world. Her body of work consists mainly of oil and acrylic paintings, drawings and graphic works. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
