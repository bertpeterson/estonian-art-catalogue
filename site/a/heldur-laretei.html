Heldur Laretei

1993–1994
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Heldur Laretei studied under Paul Luhtein and Evald Okas in the graphic art department of the Estonian SSR State Art Institute, which he graduated from together with Peeter Ulas, Herald Eelma, Silvia Liiberg and Olav Maran in 1959. Laretei's diploma work was a series of woodcuts for Shakespeare's "Hamlet". In the 1960s–1970s he practised free graphic art. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
