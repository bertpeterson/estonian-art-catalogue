Sirje Eelma

1950– · Estonian printmaker (born 1950)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Pärnu Muuseum

member of Estonian Artists' Association · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Sirje Eelma's (b. 1950 in Tallinn) main source of art is nature. For her, the printmaking process of graphic art and the nuances arising from the internal possibilities of the means of expression are important. The subdued colouring brings out well the inner glow of the work and the contrasts of different textures. References to recognisable motifs are intertwined with an elegant formal language, the mysterious technical multilayeredness of the images creates playful spatial associations and holds together a positive mood.
