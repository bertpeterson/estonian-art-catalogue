Lola Tehver


Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Lola has completed further studies in classical painting at the Estonian Academy of Arts (2018). Since 2020 she has been a member of the Estonian Painters' Association. Since 2016 she has held several solo exhibitions, and has taken part in numerous group exhibitions since 2014. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
