Wilhelm Siegfried Stavenhagen

1814–1881 · Baltic German sculptor and painter (1814-1881)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Narva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Sculptor and draughtsman, known primarily as the author of views of Estonia, Livonia and Courland in the steel engraving technique. Born in Kuldīga as the son of a merchant and owner of a lending library, he worked as a land surveyor and cartographer. After studying in Germany, he worked as a sculptor at home from 1849, drawing outdoors in nature during the summers. In 1857 he began publishing a series of steel engravings, which appeared in 10 fascicles, each containing 3 sheets. The engravings were published as albums, printed in Darmstadt, together with texts, in three volumes in 1866–67. In 1913 a reproduction album of Stavenhagen's works was published in Tallinn (39 drawings and 4 sculptures, publisher Carl Meissner). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
