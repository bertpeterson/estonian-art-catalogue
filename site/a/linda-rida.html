Linda Rida

1910–1947 · Estonian artist (1910-1947)
Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

Linda Rida's work is characterised by a painterly softness and a sensitive manner of handling. Combined with a colour-joyful, lively, emotionally sensitive treatment, this speaks of a life-affirming artistic reception of nature and surroundings, which deserves particular emphasis especially in the present harsh reality.
