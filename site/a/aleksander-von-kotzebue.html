Aleksander von Kotzebue

1815–1889
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

Painter of Baltic German origin (battle painter). Born as the youngest son of August von Kotzebue's 3rd marriage. Served in the Russian army, studied at the St Petersburg Academy of Arts 1837-45 (under A. Sauerweid), stayed in Paris 1847-48 as a pensioner of Nicholas I. From 1850 lived in Munich, 1851-53 travelled in Switzerland, Italy, Belgium. In 1857 became professor at the St Petersburg Academy of Arts, was also imperial court painter, honorary member of the Munich Academy of Arts.
