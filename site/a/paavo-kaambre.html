Paavo Käämbre

1984–
NOBA

Paavo Käämbre (b. 1984) is a painter who lives and works in Tallinn. He graduated from the Tartu Art College in painting and the restoration of murals (2010) and defended his master's degree in painting at the University of Tartu (2012). Since 2012 Käämbre has worked at Eesti Päevaleht. He has had solo exhibitions at Draakoni Gallery ("Jäänud", 2014), the Pärnu Town Gallery ("Sõnad on tuhk mu suus", 2013), the Tartu Science Park ("Tähtvere", 2012), and the Aatrium Gallery ("Mustamäe", 2012). Käämbre has also participated in numerous group exhibitions, such as "Noor Eesti Kunst" "Estnische Kunst zu Gast in Fürtsnewalde" in Fürstenwalde, Germany (2015), and the exhibition of Young Painter Price finalists at the Contemporary Art Centre in Vilnius (2013). Käämbre mainly cultivates an expressive abstract painting style. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
