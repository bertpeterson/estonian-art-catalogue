Varmo Pirk

1913–1980 · Estonian painter (1913-1980)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid; Pärnu Muuseum

trained at Pallas · trained at Tartu Art School

He was born in Smolensk, Russia; the family moved to Estonia in 1914. He lived on Laeva. He studied at the Pallas Art School in Tartu from 1938-40, continuing his studies until his arrest in spring 1945 (teachers included Aleksander Vardi and others). In 1944 he attempted to flee to the West together with Valve Janov and Kaja Kärner. He was held until 1954 in a prison camp in Arkhangelsk Oblast, Russia. From 1958 he lived in Tallinn, where he also died. Painter, watercolourist. From 1937-39 he was a stage designer at the Tartu Workers' Theatre. In the 1960s, like Lembit Saarts, he approached abstract painting through nature motifs. Bibl.: Eesti kunsti ajalugu 2, 1970, p. 89; Sirje Helme, Jaak Kangilaski, Lühike Eesti kunsti ajalugu. Tln: 1999, pp. 128, 144, 196 (ill. 98). See also: Enn Lillemets "Enne valguse vaheaega", exhibition at the Tartu City Museum, 2010. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
