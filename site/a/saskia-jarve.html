Saskia Järve


Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid

Saskia Järve earned a master's degree from the Estonian Academy of Arts and works mainly in the field of figurative painting. In her work Järve deals with everyday events and focuses on the complex psychology of human nature. She combines frames from personal life with elements of pop culture and folklore.
