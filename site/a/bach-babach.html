Bach Babach

1977– · Estonian artist (born 1977)
NOBA

Bach Babach is one of Estonia's first graffiti artists, who has also gained fame as a developer of hip-hop culture. Babach started out as a graffiti artist in the 1990s and has focused mainly on the stencil technique, having also made airbrush paintings. He is a graduate of the Tartu Art School and the Tartu Art College, later also working as a lecturer at the latter. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
