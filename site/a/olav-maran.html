Olav Maran

1933– · Estonian artist (born 1933)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Studied printmaking at ERKI in 1953-1959. Was an avant-gardist in the 1950s-1960s. From 1968 turned toward religious painting and painted landscapes and still lifes. Has also created collages. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
