Louis Höflinger

1827– · Baltic German artist and photographer (ca 1827–?)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

A lithographer, photographer and businessman active in Tartu from 1857-1875, originally from Germany. In 1857 he acquired the former G. F. Schlater lithography workshop in Tartu. He published views of Tartu and other prints, produced portraits and group pictures, and made much use of photolithography. L. Höflinger's photography studio was located in Tartu, on Promenaadi Street (in the Sukoffsky house). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
