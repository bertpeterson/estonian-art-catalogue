Peeter Ulas

1934–2008 · Estonian graphic artist (1934-2008)
Allee galerii; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Tallinn on 7 April 1934, died there on 15 December 2008. Studied ceramics and graphic art at ERKI in 1952-59. Worked as a teacher at Tallinn University. One of the most recognised Estonian graphic artists. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
