Voldemar Vaga

1899–1999 · Estonian art and architecture historian and teacher (1899-1999)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Gustav Adolf Grammar School · trained at University of Tartu

Art and architecture historian, lecturer, artist, studied at A. Laikmaa's studio and at the Faculty of Philosophy of the University of Tartu, which he completed in 1926 as a student of T. H. Kjellin. He made his activity as an artist public in the 1980s. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
