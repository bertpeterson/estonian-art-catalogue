Jaan Elken

1954– · Estonian artist (born 1954)
Allee galerii; Artrovert; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Teatri- ja Muusikamuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

The painting was in Rector Toomas Asser's office from 2021-2024. Universitas Tartuensis 2021 no. 6, p. 11: With the arrival of the coronavirus period, Rector Toomas Asser decided to remove a large black cabinet from his office, which he calls depressing. To decorate the freed-up wall space, he chose Jaan Elken's 1978 painting "Kusagil Eestis", which belongs to the collection of the university's art museum. "I don't know what it says to the new generation, but there are a lot of meaningful things here. Up there, for example, is 'KG', that is, KGB, and then you can see changes of direction - that something is changing in Estonia," the rector describes while looking at the painting.
