Salme Rosalie Riig

1903–1973 · Estonian sculptor and printmaker (1903–1973)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

trained at University of Tartu

He/she graduated from the Pärnu Girls' Gymnasium in 1922 and from the Stettin (Szczecin) home economics school in 1924, and worked as a home economics teacher in Tartu. In 1934 he/she graduated from the University of Tartu as an art historian, and in 1943 from the sculpture department of the Pallas art school, where he/she studied under the guidance of Anton Starkopf. He/she began exhibiting at art exhibitions already in 1938. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
