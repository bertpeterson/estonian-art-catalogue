Maryliis Teinfeldt-Grins

1993–
NOBA; Vaal galerii

Maryliis Teinfeldt-Grins (b. 1993) is mainly known as a textile artist and poet. In her creative work the artist focuses on the village landscape, storytelling and the Kadrina dialect, mainly practising embroidery and tapestry, and writing poetry in dialect. In her later works the artist deals with the ability to read landscape. Teinfeldt-Grins has studied at the Pallas University of Applied Sciences, at the Department of Textile Art of the Art Academy of Latvia, and is currently studying in the Master's programme in Contemporary Art at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Teinfeldt-Grins is a laureate of the Eduard Wiiralt Scholarship awarded by the Estonian Ministry of Culture, a laureate of the Adamson-Eric Scholarship, and the Estonian Textile Artists' Union's Young Textile Artist of 2019. The literary festival Prima Vista recognised the artist with an award for her literary texts "Esimene samm" ("The First Step"). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
