Hannah Harkes

1989–
NOBA

Hannah Harkes (1989) is a Scottish artist and printmaker who has lived and worked in Tallinn since 2012. She graduated (BA) from Aberdeen Art Academy in Scotland, in the Fine Arts department with a major in printmaking (2011), and obtained a Master's degree in Death, Religion and Culture at the University of Winchester in England (2019). She has held solo exhibitions in Estonia, Finland, Sweden and Great Britain, and has participated in group exhibitions from Xi'an to New York. In 2020 she had a solo exhibition "As long as we make it". (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
