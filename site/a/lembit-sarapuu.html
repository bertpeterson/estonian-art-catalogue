Lembit Sarapuu

1930–2024
Allee galerii; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA

In 1951 graduated from Tallinn Secondary School No. 22, followed by military service. In 1955 entered the painting department of the Estonian State Art Institute, which he graduated in 1961. 1961-1964 drawing teacher at Tallinn Secondary School No. 18, thereafter freelance artist.
