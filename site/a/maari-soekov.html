Maari Soekov


NOBA

Maari Soekov is an Estonian visual artist and illustrator, whose work brings together a dreamlike sensitivity, a nature-close worldview and mythopoeia. She has graduated from the photography specialty of the Tartu Art College (2010–2014) and furthered her education within the Erasmus programme at the Escola Superior Artística do Porto (2012–2013).
