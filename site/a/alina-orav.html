Alina Orav

1989–
NOBA; Vaal galerii

Alina Orav (b. 1989 in Tallinn) is a painting and painting-installation artist from Estonia who lives and works in Estonia and Canada. She is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Painters' Association. She obtained a master's degree in painting from the Estonian Academy of Arts in 2017, and Alina has also furthered her studies on exchange programmes in Florence (2011–2012 at ABAF University, IT) and Vancouver (2015 at ECUAD University, CA) as well as in art practices in London (UK), Amsterdam (NL) and Volda (NO). The artist has held over 20 solo exhibitions in total: in Estonia, Vancouver (CA), Helsinki (FI), Ísafjörður (IS), and has taken part in group exhibitions in Brande (DK), Berlin (DE), Florence (IT), Helsinki (FI), Moscow (RU), Stockholm (SE), Vancouver (CA), Watford (UK), Venice (USA), Japan and Estonia. She has curated group exhibitions in Vancouver (CA, 2015) and Helsinki (FI, 2018). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
