Elisa Ritsing


NOBA

Elisa Ritsing is a freelance multidisciplinary artist who creates through painting and dance. She has obtained a bachelor's degree in choreography from Tallinn University and later studied painting at the Open Academy of the Estonian Academy of Arts. In her work she focuses mainly on two themes: femininity in its various forms; and the subconscious and the resulting unconscious patterns of thought and behaviour. Ritsing's works have taken part in exhibitions in various places in Estonia: at the Pärnu New Art Museum, the ArtDepoo gallery, the Design and Architecture Gallery, the MA EI SAA ARU art festival, and elsewhere.
