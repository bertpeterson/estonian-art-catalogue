Luisa Greta Vilo


Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Luisa Greta Vilo is a photographic artist who graduated from the Pallas University of Applied Sciences. Vilo began exhibiting in 2020, when she participated in the joint exhibition of Estonian artists "Paus". Since then she has participated in several group exhibitions and created two solo exhibitions, "Loodu sees" [Inside the Created] at Aparaaditehas as part of the Open Culture Platform (in 2022) and "Haavatavuse fragmendid" [Fragments of Vulnerability] at Tampere House in Tartu (2023). Pallas has recognised Vilo with a field-specific scholarship in the arts direction. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
