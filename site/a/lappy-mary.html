Lappy Mary

1982– · Estonian painter (born 1982)
NOBA

Lappy Mary (Maria Lapteva) is an Estonian painter. She has graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in painting (MA, 2014). She is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Authors' Society, and in 2015-2017 she was the chairwoman of the board of the Estonian Painters' Association. She has participated in exhibitions since 2008. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
