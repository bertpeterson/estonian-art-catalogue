Benjamin Vassermann

1949–
NOBA

Benjamin Vassermann (b. 1949 in Kirov Oblast, Russia) graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute in graphic art (1979) and has participated in exhibitions since 1978. Vassermann has been a member of the Estonian Artists' Association since 1989 and a member of the Estonian Association of Printmakers since 1992. He practises free graphic art in intaglio printing techniques (etching, aquatint) and digital printing. He has also created watercolours, posters, book designs and has designed catalogues and record covers. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
