Jevgeni Kolpakov

1951–
NOBA

Born (1951) in Jõgeva County, grew up in Ida-Viru County. In 1980 he began studying fine art at the Moscow Art University and graduated with honours in 1985. His first solo exhibition took place at the Kohtla-Järve Oil Shale Museum in 1981, and since then he has participated every year in group exhibitions (spring and autumn exhibitions) in the White Hall of the PKM and in the former Jõhvi "Oktoober" cultural palace, whose exhibition hall was a branch of the Estonian State Museum. He has had more than 40 solo exhibitions and has participated in nearly a hundred group and joint exhibitions. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
