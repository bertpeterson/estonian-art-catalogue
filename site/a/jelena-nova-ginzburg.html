Jelena Nova-Ginzburg


NOBA

Jelena Nova is an artist-impressionist who has graduated in product design from the Estonian Academy of Arts. Jelena has been engaged in art since 2014 and her main theme is animals and birds who have ended up in unexpected and humorous situations.
