Hermann von Kügelgen

1828–1897
Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Hermann von Kügelgen (1828-1897) was the youngest child of Carl and Emilie von Kügelgen. He was born in Estonia, studied at the Tallinn Cathedral School, and received an engineering education in Dresden. After the death of his first wife Emma, he married Johanna, daughter of the Tartu artist August Matthias Hagen. Kügelgen was a teacher at the Tartu Realschule and at the art courses of the German Craftsmen's Society. He died in 1897 in Tartu. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
