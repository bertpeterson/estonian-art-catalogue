Work as a sculptor: Peeter Mudist (1942–2013) is one of the greatest Estonian painters of the 20th century, whose painterly compositions are based on realism while also presenting visionary imagery. He graduated from the Estonian SSR State Art Institute in 1967 as a painter and began creating sculptures that same year. His first painted plaster portraits and figures were succeeded in the late 1970s by granite sculptures of stylised form. In these, the author has at times worked the natural stone only minimally. He has also created strongly generalised portrait works and large-format granite figures. Peeter Mudist has been awarded the Kristjan Raud Prize (1989), the Konrad Mägi Medal (1991), and the Order of the White Star, III Class (2001).
