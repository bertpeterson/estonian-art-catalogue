Ülle Meister

1948–2021 · Estonian illustrator (1948-2021)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haapsalu ja Läänemaa Muuseumid

Ülle Meister was born on 27 August 1948 in Lüganuse, died on 6 January 2021 in Tallinn. In 1967-1973 she studied graphic art at ERKI (EKA), graduating as a book graphic artist. Her diploma work was a primer, which was in use in schools for many years. She then worked at the publishing house Valgus. Ülle Meister has designed record sleeves and postcards, created ex-libris, and worked in free graphic art and watercolour. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
