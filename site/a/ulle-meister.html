Ülle Meister

1948–2021 · Estonian illustrator (1948-2021)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haapsalu ja Läänemaa Muuseumid

Ülle Meister was born 27. VIII 1948 in Lüganuse, died 6. I 2021 in Tallinn. 1967-1973 studied graphic art at ERKI (EKA), graduated as a book graphic artist. Her diploma work was a primer that was used in schools for many years. She then worked at the publishing house Valgus. Ülle Meister has designed record sleeves and postcards, created ex libris, and worked in free graphic art and watercolour. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
