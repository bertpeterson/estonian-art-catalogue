Tarmo Roosimölder

1970–
Allee galerii; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vernissage

Tarmo Roosimölder (1970) studied geology at the University of Tartu, but as a painter and graphic artist he is self-taught, having, among other things, furthered his skills in Tõnis Laanemaa's drawing studio. His "style" has developed under the combined influence of desert trips in his school years and the native seacoast, where the small human meets great nature. Roosimölder has participated in exhibitions both in Estonia and elsewhere, and his works are in many private collections. He is a member of the Estonian Association of Independent Graphic Artists and the Estonian Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
