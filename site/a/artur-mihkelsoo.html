Artur Mihkelsoo

1902–1988 · Estonian artist (1902-1988)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

trained at Pallas

Began art studies under A. Kaigorodov. 1925-1929 studied sculpture at "Pallas". 1929-30 furthered his studies in painting and applied art in Paris at the Conservatoire National des Arts et Métiers. Sculptor, painter, applied graphic artist. Went to Germany in 1944, from 1947 lived in England, from 1952 in Toronto, Canada. Was a member of EKKKÜ in 1940 and a member of EKKT from 1961-85. Buried in Tartu. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
