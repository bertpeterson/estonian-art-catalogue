Kristen Rästas


Artrovert; NOBA

Kristen Rästas is an Estonian artist born in 1992. She is currently studying painting in the master's programme at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Kristen has taken part in exhibitions since 2012. Her preferred medium is painting, though she also works with video art. The artist's style has previously been described as "expressive pop". Kristen lives and works in Tallinn.
