Irma Holm

2000–
NOBA; Vaal galerii

Irma Holm works in the fields of photography, installation and graphic art. In creating her works she draws on personal experiences and deals with themes of mental health, femininity and everyday poetry. Holm combines humour and sensitivity to reveal the tensions arising at the border of public and private space. She has participated in group exhibitions in various places in Estonia, and her work "Moenukk Irma" has been exhibited at the Poznań Print Biennale.
