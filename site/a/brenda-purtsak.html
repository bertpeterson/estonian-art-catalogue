Brenda Purtsak

1994–
Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA; Vaal galerii

Brenda Purtsak (born 1994) is an artist from Estonia. She graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts with a degree in Contemporary Art (2022) and Painting (2020). Since 2023 she has worked as a lecturer at the Estonian Academy of Arts. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
