Tauno Ostra


Artrovert

Tauno Ostra is currently a first-year Master's student in Fine Arts, specialising in graphic art. He considers his most important exhibitions to be the solo exhibition "Identiteet", which took place in 2015 at the Haapsalu City Gallery and in 2016 at the Võru City Gallery, and "Sümmeetria" in February–March 2017 at the Viljandi City Gallery. Among his major group exhibitions are the 5th Tallinn International Drawing Triennial and the Spring Exhibition (2016) at the Tallinn Art Hall, and the Baltic Drawing Biennial in Imatra, Finland. In addition, this year's Mirror Face To Face at the National Library and in Vicenza, Italy. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
