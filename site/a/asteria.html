Asteria

1991–
NOBA

Asteria (b. 1991) is an Estonian painter who lives and works in Tartu. She graduated from the Tartu Art College in decoration and earned a degree in painting from the Pallas Higher Art School. Asteria's works are regularly exhibited at various exhibitions and can be found in numerous private collections both in Estonia and abroad.
