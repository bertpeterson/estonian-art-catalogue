Viive Noor

1955–
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Viive Noor (1955) studied at the Estonian State Art Institute and has, since 1978, participated in more than 400 exhibitions in 33 countries, from America to Japan. She has had 96 solo exhibitions in total. She works with graphic art, book illustration as well as exhibition curating. In her work Noor deals mainly with universal human themes, but also touches on ecology and world events. Noor's work and activities have received numerous accolades, including the Order of the White Star, 5th Class. She is the initiator and organiser of the Tallinn Illustration Triennial and a member of the Estonian Association of Graphic Designers and the Estonian Artists' Association.
