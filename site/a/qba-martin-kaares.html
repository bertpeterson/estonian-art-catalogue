QBA Martin Kaares


NOBA

In 2004 she obtained a bachelor's degree at EKA in the speciality of scenography. In 2018 she joined the Estonian Painters' Association, and since 2019 she has also been a member of the Estonian Artists' Association. Colourful and lush in style, she has been actively engaged in creative work since 2008. In 2019 she received the Grand Prize of the Jurmala Seascape Biennial.
