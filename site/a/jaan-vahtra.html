Jaan Vahtra

1882–1947 · Estonian artist, writer, journalist (1882-1947)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

J. Vahtra was mobilised into the army during the First World War, during the St. Petersburg period, but did not end up at the front. He took an active part in the activities of Estonian students in St. Petersburg, in 1914-1915 he helped edit the handwritten art journal "Ronk", in 1915-1916 he belonged to the union of students interested in art, music and literature "Ritsikate ring", where the writers Johannes Semper and Karl Ast-Rumor were also active. He moved from St. Petersburg to Estonia in June 1918. In 1923 he was a founding member of the Estonian Artists' Group (member of the group until 1925). In 1926 he joined the art association Pallas.
