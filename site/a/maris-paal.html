Maris Paal


Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Maris Paal is an artist living and working in Tallinn. She graduated from the painting department of the Pallas Higher Art School and is currently studying for a master's degree in contemporary art at the Estonian Academy of Arts. She has had solo exhibitions at the Tartu Art House, at the Põhjala factory (together with Brita Maripuu), at the Türi Culture Centre (together with Talia and Helen Bunder) and at the Jakobi Gallery.
