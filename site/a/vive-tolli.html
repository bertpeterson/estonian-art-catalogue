Vive Tolli

1928–2020 · Estonian printmaker and artist (1928-2020)
Allee galerii; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstiakadeemia muuseum

member of Artists' Union of the USSR · member of Estonian Artists' Association · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Tallinn, studied ceramics 1947-49 at the Tallinn Institute of Applied Art and graphics 1949-53 at ERKI. Has worked as a teacher at the art academy, professor since 1992. Honoured Artist of the ESSR in 1965. One of the most outstanding, prolific and internationally best-known Estonian graphic artists, mainly in the field of intaglio printing. Has also created ex libris and illustrations, mostly for Estonian poems. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
