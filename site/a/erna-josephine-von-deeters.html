Erna Josephine von Deeters

1876–1961
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

He exhibited at exhibitions in Saint Petersburg and Moscow at the exhibitions of the artists' association "Novoe Obšestvo" and in the salons Mir Isskustva and Obšestvo Nezavissimõh. He also exhibited in Kiev, Helsinki, Riga, Rome and Venice. Later in Tallinn, Tartu, Lübeck and Königsberg. He took part in exhibitions organized by the artists' associations ARS and EKKKÜ and by the Cultural Endowment. He was a member of the Estonian Academic Association of Professional Artists (Eesti Akadeemiliste Kutseõiguslike Kunstnikkude Koondis) and of the Helsinki Russian Artists' Union.
