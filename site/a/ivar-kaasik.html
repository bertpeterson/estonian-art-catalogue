Ivar Kaasik

1965– · Estonian painter (1965-)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vernissage

Ivar Kaasik (b. 1965 in Kuressaare) is an Estonian painter and jewelry artist. Kaasik began his studies in the Department of Architecture at the Estonian State Art Institute, but graduated from the university as a metal artist. Since 1992, Kaasik has lived in Berlin. In 1989–1990, he studied at the Halle University of Art and Design in Germany. Since 2001, Kaasik has been a member of the German Association of Painters.
