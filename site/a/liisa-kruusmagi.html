Liisa Kruusmägi

1988– · Estonian painter (born 1988)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Liisa Kruusmägi was born in Tallinn in 1988. She studied painting at the bachelor's level at the Estonian Academy of Arts and completed her master's degree in drawing in 2013. Kruusmägi has furthered her studies at the Rhode Island School of Design, and has taken part in residency programmes in Vancouver at James Black Gallery, at AIRY, and at Yosuga in Japan, as well as at the Kitokia Grafika risograph printing studio in Lithuania. She has held solo exhibitions in Portugal, Sweden, Canada, Japan, the USA, Lithuania, Latvia, Germany and Estonia. In addition to those mentioned, she has also taken part in group exhibitions in London, Berlin, Slovakia, Italy, Finland and Russia. Liisa Kruusmägi is a freelance artist who mainly works with the media of painting and drawing and works as an illustrator. She has been a member of the Artists' Union since 2013.
