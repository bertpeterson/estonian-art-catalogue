Kati Müüripeal

1994–
Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Kati Müüripeal (1994) is a painter active in Estonia who has taken part in group exhibitions since 2019. As a teenager she acquired academic drawing skills from the animal painter Sándor Martin Sterl. She has now graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts with a BA in Fine Arts and an MA in Contemporary Art. During an exchange programme she furthered her studies at the University of Fine Arts Hamburg. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
