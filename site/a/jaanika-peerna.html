Jaanika Peerna

1971–
Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii

Jaanika Peerna (1971) is an artist born in Tallinn who has lived and worked in New York since 1998. She completed her master's studies in 2005 at the State University of New York. Her works, brimming with the forces of nature, encompass drawings, installations and, increasingly, performance art of very different scales. Peerna's works can be found in hundreds of private and public collections, and she is represented in the USA by JHB Fine Art and ARC Fine Art. In 2018, the Glynn Vivian museum in the UK purchased for its collection Peerna's performance titled Glacier Elegy. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
