Lauri Koppel

1986–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA; Ruki galerii; Vaal galerii

Lauri Koppel (b. 1986) is a young printmaker who has graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts with a bachelor's and master's degree in printmaking. Koppel has participated in a number of group exhibitions and in 2010 he was awarded the Eduard Wiiralt scholarship given out by the Ministry of Culture. Since 2013 Koppel has been a member of the Estonian Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
