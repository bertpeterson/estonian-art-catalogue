Lydia Mei

1896–1965 · Estonian artist (1896-1965)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid; Narva Muuseum

In 1915 she graduated with a silver medal from the Tallinn Girls' Crown Gymnasium, then studied in 1915–1916 at J. Bagayev's architecture courses in Petersburg and in 1916–1918 architecture at the Petrograd Women's Polytechnic Institute. In 1918–1919 she worked as an assistant architect at the Tallinn City Government. In 1919 she first appeared at an art exhibition of the Pallas art society in Tartu and furthered her training in painting under Nikolai Triik. In 1920–1921 she worked as a drawing teacher at the Tartu Teachers' Seminary. In 1922–1923 she stayed repeatedly in sanatoriums in Germany. From 1925 she took part in exhibitions in Estonia, as well as in Helsinki, Riga, Berlin, Amsterdam and elsewhere. From 1925 she was a member of the Central Union of Estonian Fine Artists, in 1931–1935 a member of the Estonian Artists' Group, in 1936 a member of the Applied Artists' Union, and from 1946 a member of the ESSR Artists' Union.
