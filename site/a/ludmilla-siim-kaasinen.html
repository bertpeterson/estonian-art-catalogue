Ludmilla Siim-Kaasinen


Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA; Vernissage

Ludmilla studied at Tartu 2nd Secondary School and then at Tartu Art School. She graduated from ERKI in 1965 in painting. She then worked as an art teacher at Tallinn 46th Secondary School, influencing future painters, for example Ando Keskküla was her student. She was interested in spiritualist and mystical teachings.
