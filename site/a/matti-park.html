Matti Pärk

1945–
Haus Galerii; NOBA

Matti Pärk (b. 1945) has graduated from the Tartu Art School in the speciality of woodworking and from ERKI in the speciality of book illustrator. He has worked as the head of the art editorial department of the ENE [Estonian Soviet Encyclopaedia] and as a lecturer at the drawing department of EKA (from 1984 to the present). He has presented his drawings and pastel paintings at numerous solo exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad, and has also participated in group exhibitions. He has designed, laid out and illustrated nearly 200 books (several of which have received recognition at competitions). At the last drawing triennial, Matti Pärk was one of the nominees for the main prize. He is currently working on preparing a solo exhibition for the Kuressaare Town Hall Gallery.
