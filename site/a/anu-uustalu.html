Anu Uustalu

1914–2011 · Estonian artist (1914-2011)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

trained at Pallas

Anu-Leenu Uustalu was born in Tallinn into a schoolteacher's family. In 1940–1941 she worked as a freelance artist in Tallinn, in 1944 she fled to Germany, where she worked as an artist for the American Red Cross and as a drawing teacher at an Estonian elementary school and gymnasium. In 1949 the artist moved together with her sister Benita Uustalu to the United States, to the city of Milwaukee, Wisconsin. She worked as a technical draughtswoman, and from 1977 worked as a freelance artist. In 1968 all her earlier work was destroyed in a fire. In 1979 A. Uustalu designed a clothing display introducing Estonian folk culture in the permanent exhibition of the Milwaukee Public Museum. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
