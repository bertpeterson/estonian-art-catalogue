Kadri Krüüner


NOBA

Kadri Krüüner graduated from the painting department of Pallas in 2022 and from the specialty of teacher of arts and technology at the University of Tartu in 2024. Kadri teaches art and gathers inspiration at Tartu Veeriku School and keeps her brushes busy in her free time. She is interested in soul, city, cultural, micro and macro landscapes and the clashing sounds of colours. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
