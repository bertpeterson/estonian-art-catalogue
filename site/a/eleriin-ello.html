Eleriin Ello

1984–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Eleriin Ello (1984) is a freelance painter who lives and works in Tallinn. After brief studies in the painting department of the University of Tartu, she earned a bachelor's degree in graphic art at the Estonian Academy of Arts (2007) and a master's degree in painting (2010). During her master's studies she furthered her education at the painting department of the University of Porto in Portugal. Eleriin Ello's work has been recognised with third place in the Baltic Young Painter Prize in 2012 and the Sadolin Art Prize in 2016. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
