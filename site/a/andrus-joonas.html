Andrus Joonas

1970– · Estonian artist (1970-2021)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

Andrus Joonas (1970-2021) was a performance artist, painter and installation artist. Joonas has been connected with the Estonian Academy of Arts college Academia Grata both as a student and as a lecturer; as a lecturer he has also worked at Academia Non Grata.
