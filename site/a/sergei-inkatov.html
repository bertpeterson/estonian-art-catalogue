Sergei Inkatov

1971– · artist (fl. 2006)
Allee galerii; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Narva Muuseum; Vernissage

Sergei Inkatov was born in 1971 in Baku, studied at the Baku Art School 1987–1989, at the Azim Zadeh Art School in Baku 1989–1992, and at the Venetsianov Art School in 1994. He lives in Tallinn. Recognitions: 2006 Diploma of Excellence, Global Art Annual, London, United Kingdom; 2010 laureate of the "Cultural Property" foundation, Russia; 2011 laureate of the Tallinn – European Capital of Culture art competition; 2017 member of the Mondial Academia, France; 2017 Mondial Academia Ambassador in Estonia; 2017 Gold Medal "Prix international des Galeries" in the category of abstract painting, France; 2019 1st place at the "Artweeks France" competition in Miramas; 2019 Gold Medal "Prix international des Galeries" in the category of abstract painting, France. Since 2020 member of the Estonian Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
