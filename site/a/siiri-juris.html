Siiri Jüris

1992– · Estonian painter (born 1992)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tütar galerii; Vaal galerii

Siiri Jüris is an artist living and working in Tartu. She graduated from the Tartu Art School in the specialty of decorator-stylist (2012) and obtained both a bachelor's and a master's degree from the Painting Department of the University of Tartu (2015 and 2017 respectively). Since her bachelor's thesis, Jüris has repeatedly turned to portrait painting, being interested in the possibilities and possibility of depicting the individual and their states, while consistently questioning the conventions and traditions of this painting genre. Jüris's paintings are held in several private collections. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
