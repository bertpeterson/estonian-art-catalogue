Jane Kukk


NOBA

Jane Kukk is an Estonian visual artist and designer living in Las Vegas, Nevada. Her work is a visual collage of personal observations and intimate symbols, reflecting a deep attachment to the seeming emptiness and peace of the Southwestern desert.
