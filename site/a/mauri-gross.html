Mauri Gross

1969–
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii; Vaal galerii; Vernissage

Mauri Gross (b. 1969 in Rapla) received his art education at Tallinn University and additionally studied stage design at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Between 1998 and 2007 he worked as a lecturer in graphic design at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Since 2009 Gross has been a freelance artist, expressing himself mainly through oil painting. His other fields of activity are graphic design and interior design. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
