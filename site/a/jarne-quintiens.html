Jarne Quintiens

1994–
NOBA

Jarne Quintiens (born 1994) lives and works in Ghent, Belgium where he is currently completing his Master at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts. His current work is focused on drawing, collage and décollages that seem to center around comercialistic imagery. They express themes of repetition, fragility and decay while also raising questions to our visual culture. In 2017 he enjoyed studying at the Estonian Academy of Arts in Tallinn.
