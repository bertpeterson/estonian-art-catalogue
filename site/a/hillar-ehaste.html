Hillar EHASTE


NOBA

Hillar Ehaste (b. 1954, Võru) is an Estonian artist working in Tartu, who took up painting in mature age after a long career as a mining engineer. He graduated from the Tallinn Polytechnic Institute in 1977 and improved his skills in painting at the Tartu People's University and at the SUVI art studio under the guidance of Robert Suvi.
