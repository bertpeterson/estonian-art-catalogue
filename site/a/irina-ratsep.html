Irina Rätsep

1946–2016 · Estonian sculptor (1946-2016)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Moscow into a family of intellectuals with Russian and Polish roots, he arrived in Tallinn with his lawyer parents in 1956. Having finished Tallinn Secondary School No. 19, he entered the Estonian State Art Institute, completing his studies in 1972 as a sculptor.
