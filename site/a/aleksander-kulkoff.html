Aleksander Kulkoff

1889–1970
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Kulkoff created landscape paintings in the style of Russian Art Nouveau symbolism, and also painted portraits of society figures and artists as well as theatrical religious-themed paintings. Kulkoff received his art education in Moscow at the School of Painting, Sculpture and Architecture (and was awarded the title of First-Class Artist).
