Karl von Hoerschelmann

1899–1951 · Baltic German artist (1899-1951)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

In 1922 he came to Estonia, where he initially worked as an unskilled laborer at a sawmill, later as a draftsman at the Ministry of Agriculture and as a draughtsman at a textile factory. He illustrated books and designed theatre sets and postcards. He took part in local Russian literary societies. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
