Arnold Akberg

1894–1984 · Estonian painter (1894-1984)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

From 1920 worked as a drawing teacher in Tallinn. Joined the Estonian Artists' Group in 1925, in 1927 travelled to Berlin and Paris. In 1928 exhibited at the "Great Berlin Art Exhibition" together with Blumenfeldt, Hiist, Laarman and Olvi. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
