Helena Lepik

1994–
NOBA

Helena Lepik, (b. 1994) is a freelance painter who lives and works in Tallinn. She studied at the Estonian Academy of Arts, Faculty of Fine Arts, BA in Painting (2017). Her work is directed towards the problematics of psychological self-search, sexuality and the determination of identity, as well as themes related to the soul's journey arising from death. She prefers to work in the format of large flat painting, paying attention to a forceful sense of form and colour. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
