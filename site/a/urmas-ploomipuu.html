Urmas Ploomipuu

1942–1990 · Estonian painter and printmaker (1942-1990)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

1989 at the competition for banknote designs announced by the Council of Ministers of the Estonian SSR, and U. Ploomipuu received third prize and his design was used to print the Estonian 1- and 2-kroon notes. The latter were issued in 1992, 2006, 2007 and were in circulation until the transition to the euro in 2011. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
