Andres Koort

1969–
NOBA; Ruki galerii

Andres Koort (b. 1969 in Tallinn) is an Estonian painter, scenographer, designer and curator. He graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in stage design (1987–1993) and furthered his studies in painting at the Academia Minerva in Groningen, Netherlands. Koort has participated in exhibitions since 1991 and has served as a board member of the Estonian Painters' Association since 2002. He has created stage designs for productions at the Estonian Drama Theatre, Tallinn City Theatre, Endla Theatre in Pärnu, the Estonian Puppet Theatre and Theatrum. The artist's works can be found in the collections of the Tartu Art Museum and the Lithuanian Art Museum. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
