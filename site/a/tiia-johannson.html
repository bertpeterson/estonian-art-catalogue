Tiia Johannson

1965–2002
Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Tiia Johannson was a media artist, lecturer and researcher. She began as a watercolourist and painter. She has been considered one of the representatives of neo-expressionism in Estonian art of the 1980s. From 1990 she worked mainly on numerous media art projects dealing with video, multimedia and information society technologies. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
