Johann Köler

1826–1899 · Estonian artist (1826-1899)
Allee galerii; Balti Oksjon; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Maaelumuuseumid SA, C. R. Jakobsoni Talumuuseum

trained at Imperial Academy of Arts

In 1848 enrolled in the evening courses of the St. Petersburg Academy of Arts and in 1851 became a full-fledged student, which he completed in the field of history painting (diploma work "Hercules Brings Cerberus from the Gates of Hell"). After his studies he continued his education abroad. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
