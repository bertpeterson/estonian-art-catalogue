Herkki Erich Merila

1964–
Allee galerii; Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA

Herkki Erich Merila (b. 1964 in Tallinn) is, as a member of DeStudio, the father of Estonian contemporary photographic art. And, as befits a classic, he is drawn to the most eternal of all possible motifs: the female body. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
