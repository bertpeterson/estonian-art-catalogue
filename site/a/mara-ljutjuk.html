Mara Ljutjuk

1978– · Estonian painter and curator (born 1978)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii; NOBA

Mara Ljutjuk was born in Tallinn in 1978. She graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in painting in 2001, and also obtained a master's degree in education sciences there. She furthered her education in fine arts in 2007 at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia in Spain. Since 2006 she has been a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Painters' Association. Since 2004 she has held several solo exhibitions and has participated in numerous group exhibitions since 2000. In addition to painting, Mara is engaged in curating and designing exhibitions, and is actively involved in curating and organising the programme of the Evald Okas Museum. In 2019 she received the Galerii-G art award. In 2020 she was a nominee for the Konrad Mägi Prize. Mara has three children (b. 2009/2012/2015) and lives and works in Tallinn.
