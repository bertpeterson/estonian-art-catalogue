Danel Kahar

1990–
NOBA; Vaal galerii

Danel Kahar (b.1990) is an artist who lives and works in Tallinn. He graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts, Department of Fine Arts, majoring in painting, BA (2016). His first solo exhibition took place in 2017 at Draakoni Gallery; the same year he also had a large-scale exhibition together with Grisli Soppe-Kahar in the great hall of the Tartu Art House. In the summer of 2018 he was in residence together with Grisli Soppe-Kahar in Italy (Mazzano Romano). His most recent solo exhibition took place in 2021 at Gallery Metropolis, where he presented objects in which painting is blended with assemblage and sculpture.
