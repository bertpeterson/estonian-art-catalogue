Endel Bergmann

1919–1983 · Estonian sculptor (1919-1983)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Singer (baritone); ETL 1945; 1930 Pärnu Boys' Gymnasium; 1938 TK (A. Arder's class); was a soloist of the Vienna Volksoper in 1938–39, of the Kassel State Opera in 1942–1943, and of "Estonia" in 1940–41 and 1944–1988; worked from 1940, with interruptions, as a lecturer at TK; professor from 1947; performed outside Estonia; was also known as a chamber and oratorio singer; also appeared in film, and several films were made about him; first place at the international singing competition in Vienna in 1938; Honoured Artist of the ESSR 1945; People's Artist of the ESSR 1952; People's Artist of the USSR 1954; Soviet Estonian Prize in 1949, 1959, 1967; since 1993 a fund named after him has operated at the Estonia Society to support voice students, and singing competitions are held (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
