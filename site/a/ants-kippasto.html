Ants Kippasto


NOBA; Tartu Linnamuuseum

Kippasto has been engaged in art for 25 years and is the winner of the 2016 linocut competition. In addition to Estonia, she has sold her graphic art to Japan, America, Mexico, Ireland and elsewhere. Accolades LAULUPIDU (SONG FESTIVAL) (2014, linocut), TYPA linocut competition 2016 winning work Participation in exhibitions 2003 Tartu vaade (View of Tartu). E-kunstisalong (E-art salon) 2016 Linocut competition and exhibition. TYPA. winning work Laulupidu (Song Festival) 2018 Linocut competition and exhibition. TYPA 2018 Tartu Art Annual Exhibition 2018. Tartu Art House 2021 Linocut competition and exhibition. TYPA 2023 THKS 25 years of activity retrospective exhibition. St. John's Church 2023 New Art Museum Recycling exhibition GIVE NEW LIFE.
