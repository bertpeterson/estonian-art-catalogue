Margus Kontus

1987–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii; NOBA

Margus Kontus (1987) has graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts' Contemporary Art curriculum (MA2020). Has participated in several group exhibitions and light festivals. Margus Kontus's work is characterised by the interpretation of magnetism through art. His works are like tiny meditations that seek connections between the visible and the invisible world. Since 2020 he has belonged to the art group DARKovSKY. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
