Eveli Varik


Allee galerii; NOBA

Eveli Varik has graduated from the graphic art department of the Estonian Academy of Arts. Has also studied design and spatial design. Has gained recognition as an interior and object designer. Has curated and designed exhibitions since 1999. Varik worked for nine years as chief artist and gallerist at Rakvere Theatre. Currently a teacher of the history of style and elective subjects at Tartu Art School. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
