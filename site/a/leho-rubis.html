Leho Rubis


NOBA

Leho Rubis completed his bachelor's studies in painting at the University of Tartu in 2001 and his master's studies in art history in 2010. In his work Rubis delves into the relationships between consciousness, text and fields of perception. Rubis's solo exhibitions have taken place in Estonia, India and Egypt. In 2010 he curated the joint exhibition of Estonian artists „A Gift to Arvo Pärt“. From 2005 to 2023 he was the artistic director of the cultural festivals „Kolmkõla“ and „PaIDA“. Since 2021 he has been the author of the Estonian Classical Radio programme series „Raaga aeg“. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
