Gottlieb Welté

1745–1792 · German painter (1745-1792)
Allee galerii; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum

Gottlieb Welté was born in the late 1740s in the Mainz/Frankfurt region of Germany, arrived in Estonia in 1782, where he worked at Põltsamaa Castle, in Tallinn, at Lohu Manor and elsewhere. He died on 17 December 1792 at Lohu Manor. He was an etcher, fresco painter and musician. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
