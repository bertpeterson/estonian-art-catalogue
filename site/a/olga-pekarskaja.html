Olga Pekarskaja

1984–
NOBA

Pekarskaja (b. 1984) is an artist working in Tallinn, whose creative development is closely tied to the constructivist painting school. She graduated from Tallinn University in 2011 (Master's degree) and, between 2005 and 2014, took part in the constructive painting workshop led by artist N. Litvinova, which shaped the foundations of her handwriting and compositional approach. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
