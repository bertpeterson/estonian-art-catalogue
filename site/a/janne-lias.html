Janne Lias

1981–
NOBA

Janne Lias (b. 1981) is an interdisciplinary artist working in Tallinn. She graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts with a degree in painting (2019) and completed a master's degree in contemporary art (2022). She previously earned a master's degree in informatics at Tallinn University of Technology, and therefore often combines information technology with art in her work.
