Aleksander Ipsberg

1909–1944 · Estonian sculptor (1909-1944)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts · trained at Pallas

Estonian actor, director and theatre manager, studied at the Tallinn town school, graduated in 1916 as an external student from Tallinn Realschule. He furthered his education in 1908 at Emanuel Reicher's theatre courses in Berlin, later going on study trips to St Petersburg, Paris and Brussels. From 1899 to 1906 he was a clerical official at the Tallinn district court and city administration, and performed with the Estonia amateur theatre troupe, and in 1906 also at the Tallinn German Theatre. He was one of the founders of the professional Estonia theatre (1906) and directed and acted there until 1914. In 1910, together with Theodor Altermann, he published the journal Näitelava. He performed in the summer of 1913 at the Kiel City Theatre and in 1914–15 at the theatres of Nizhny Novgorod, Kazan, Samara, Saratov and the Nikitsky theatre in Moscow. From 1915–20 he served as a military official (captain) in Tallinn, while also performing at Estonia, and from 1918–21 was the commandant, actor and director of the Drama Theatre, and from 1921–22 its director. He worked at Estonia in 1922–23, 1927–41 and 1944–49, founded his eponymous People's Theatre in 1923, and led it until 1927. From 1919–39 he also occasionally performed at the Tallinn Russian Theatre, Endla, Ugala, Vanemuine, the Narva Theatre and the Kuressaare Theatre. He was mobilised as a reserve officer into the Red Army in 1941, and from 1942–44 belonged to the State Art Ensembles of the Estonian SSR.
