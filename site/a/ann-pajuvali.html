Ann Pajuväli

1990–
Kogo galerii; NOBA; Vaal galerii

Ann Pajuväli (1990) works mainly with drawing and installation, capturing quiet moments that in their ordinariness remain unnoticed. Moving between the familiar and the abstract, she reduces objects to poetic fragments, playing at the edge of recognisability. Pajuväli's work is guided by intuition rather than a fixed narrative, creating works with a dreamlike quality. She creates a world where time seems to stretch — a meditative space that is at once personal and universal. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
