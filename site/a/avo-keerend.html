Avo Keerend (1920–2012) was an Estonian graphic artist and painter whose creative career spanned more than six decades. He began his art studies as early as 1936, initially focusing on graphic art, but later also developed his painting skills. In his earlier work, Keerend mainly used intaglio printing techniques, creating naturalistic still lifes, nudes, and genre scenes. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
