Carl August Schwerdgeburth

1785–1878 · German painter (1785-1878)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Not particularly significant as an engraver, but he engraved the last portrait of Goethe during the writer's lifetime. (It was only completed after the writer's death.) This portrait and the cycle of steel engravings of M. Luther's life are his most important works. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
