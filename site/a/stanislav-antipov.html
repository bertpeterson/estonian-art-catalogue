Stanislav Antipov

1976–
NOBA

Stanislav Antipov (b. 1976 in Izhkar, Udmurtia) graduated from the art department of Udmurt State University in 1998 and obtained a master's degree at Tallinn University in 2008. Since 1996 he has participated in more than 50 solo and group exhibitions in Udmurtia, Estonia and Finland. He is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Painters' Association. Stanislav Antipov's works can be found in the J. Laidoner War Museum, the Estonian Health Care Museum, the Kihnu Museum, the Udmurt State Museum, the Pushkin State Museum, and in private collections in Estonia, Russia, Germany, Spain, the Czech Republic, the USA, France, Switzerland, Italy and the United Kingdom.
