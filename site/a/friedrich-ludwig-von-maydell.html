Friedrich Ludwig von Maydell

1795–1846
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

Born according to the old calendar on 29 November 1795 at Teenuse (Stenhusen) manor in Läänemaa, studied 1804-1810 at the Herrnhuter school in Neuwied and 1811-1812 at the Tallinn Cathedral School. Served 1812-1820 in the Russian army, worked 1820-1822 in the archive of Tartu University, studied under the guidance of C. A. Senff at the university's drawing school. In 1822 travelled in Berlin and Dresden, until 1827 furthered his education in art in Italy. In 1824 met the artist A. L. Richter and in 1825 travelled together with him, staying in Civitella. Lived 1827-1829 at Teenuse manor, in 1829 settled in Tartu, where in 1835 he founded a wood-engraving workshop. Began a series of copper engravings (+etchings) devoted to the history of Livonia, "Viiskümmend pilti..." ("Fifty Pictures..."), of which the 1st issue appeared in 1839 and the 2nd issue in 1842, along with two further sheets. Produced graphic teaching aids, painted portraits and altarpieces (1835 Saarde church, 1837 Ruhja church, 1845 Põlva church). Maydell's paintings for Tartu St John's Church have been destroyed. He was also a sculptor, and a designer of silverwork and woodwork. From 1829 he worked on the restoration of Tallinn's St Olaf's Church (completed 1840). Died according to the old calendar on 6 September 1846 in Tallinn, buried in the Kullamaa churchyard. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
