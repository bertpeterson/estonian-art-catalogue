Inga Heamägi

1961– · Estonian graphic artist and printmaker (born 1961)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Inga Heamägi (b. 1961 in Viljandi) has graduated from Tallinna Kunstiülikool, majoring in graphic art. She is a member of the Eesti Vabagraafikute Ühendus and a member of the Eesti Kunstnike Liit. She has held solo exhibitions in Estonia, Latvia, Finland. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
