Kaisa Puustak (born 1945 in Tallinn) is a recognised Estonian artist whose work focuses on free graphic art. She graduated in 1969 from the Estonian State Art Institute in the field of graphic art (MA). Since 1973, Puustak has actively participated in exhibitions, she is an honorary member of both the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Print Society.
