Lorenz Heinrich Petersen

1805–1895
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Active in the 19th century in Tallinn as a painter, drawing teacher, art dealer and publisher of lithographs. His best-known work is the series of lithographs "Album von Reval" (max 29 different sheets). The lithographs were printed by F. Schwabe in Berlin.
