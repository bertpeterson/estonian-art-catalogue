Jüri Arrak

1936–2022 · Estonian artist (1936–2022)
Allee galerii; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

member of ANK '64 · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

He worked as an artist at the Tallinn Metal Products Factory and in 1968–1969 as a production designer at Tallinnfilm; from 1969 a freelance artist. Merited Artist of the Estonian SSR (1988). Member of the Estonian Artists' Union from 1969. In 1996 he was professor of liberal arts at the University of Tartu. A member of the group ANK'64.
