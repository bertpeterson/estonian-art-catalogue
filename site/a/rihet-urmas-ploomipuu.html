Rihet-Urmas Ploomipuu

1942–1990
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Author of the 1- and 2-kroon banknotes of the Republic of Estonia. Graduated from Tartu Art School in 1962, and from the Estonian State Art Institute in 1970. Worked at the Estonian State Publishing House, the ARS Art Production Combine, and the Art Fund. A freelance artist since 1978. Born in Võru.
