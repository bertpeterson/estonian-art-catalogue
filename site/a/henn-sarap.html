Henn Sarap

1906–1972 · Estonian printmaker (1906-1972)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Pallas

Was born into a craftsman's family. Worked until 1940 in various print shops in Tartu. Cultivated free graphic art, book and applied graphic art, ex libris and poster art. Participated in exhibitions from 1929. Out of a strong sense of mission, he shared his knowledge of graphic art with many. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
