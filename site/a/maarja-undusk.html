Maarja Undusk

1959– · Estonian poet and leatherworker (born 1959)
Hiiumaa Muuseumid; NOBA

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Applied art. Created large-scale bindings and objects with a forceful composition and colour treatment, in her own invented hand-sewing and paper-relief technique, used papier-mâché (bindings "Armunud naise päevik" 1987, "Kaotatud paradiis" 1991, "Kurb kirjutaja" 1999, object "Neeger" 1995). Illustrated books (E. Niit's "Onu ööbiku ööpäev" 1998). Published the poetry collection "Tõsimäng" and articles on applied art. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
