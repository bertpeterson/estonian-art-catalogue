Aleksander Mülber

1897–1931 · Estonian painter (1897–1931)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Studied in 1913–1916 at the Estonian Art Society and at Ants Laikmaa's studio school. In 1917–1918, together with B. Tomasberg, O. Kallis and A. Krims, founded the society Vikerla. In 1919 contributed to the rescue of art treasures and collected the legacy of B. Tomasberg, who had fallen in the War of Independence. In 1920 his 10-sheet album of linocuts Primeurs was published in Tartu. In 1921 he moved to Paris, where he made a living from odd jobs. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
