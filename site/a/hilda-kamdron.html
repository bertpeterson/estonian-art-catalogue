Hilda Kamdron

1900–1972 · Estonian artist (1901-1972)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Pallas

After interruptions, as a graphic artist in 1946. He was admitted to the ESSR Artists' Union that same year, but was soon expelled from it. He lived withdrawn in Tartu, but, without appearing at exhibitions, painted a great deal. His work is characterised by: assemblage, collage, watercolour, precise drawings for scientific publications, etc. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
