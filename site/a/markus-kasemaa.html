Markus Kasemaa

1972– · Estonian artist (born 1972)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

Markus Kasemaa (born 28 August 1972 in Tallinn) is an Estonian artist known for his innovative and diverse art projects. He studied in 1990–1991 in the sculpture department of the Tartu Art School and in 1991–1995 in the painting department of the University of Tartu.
