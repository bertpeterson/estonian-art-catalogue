Kadi-Maarja Võsu

1992–
NOBA

Kadi-Maarja Võsu (b. 1992 in Jõgeva) is a young artist whose field of activity is mainly installation art, but she also incorporates painting, video art, photography, performance, as well as poetry and other text-based projects and music into her work. Having a clear vision of what she wants to do, she does not limit herself to just one medium. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
