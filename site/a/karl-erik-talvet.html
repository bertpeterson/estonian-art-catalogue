Karl-Erik Talvet

1991–
NOBA

Karl-Erik Talvet (b. 1991 in Paris) lives and works in Tallinn. In 2018 he completed his master's studies in painting at the Estonian Academy of Arts, and has also spent time abroad during the 2016/2017 academic year at the Brera Academy of Fine Arts in Milan. In 2019 Talvet went on a military mission to the Republic of Mali, where he served as an interpreter, and has used the experience gained there in his subsequent works and exhibition. Talvet mainly works with painting and comics and has participated in numerous exhibitions. He is a member of both the Estonian Painters' Association and the Estonian Comics Society.
