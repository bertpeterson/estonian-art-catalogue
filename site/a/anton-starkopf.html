Anton Starkopf

1889–1966 · Estonian sculptor and painter (1889-1966)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Sculptor and educator; received his art education in 1911-12 in Munich at A. Azbe's art school; 1912-13 in Paris at the Academie Russe and the Academie de la Grande Chaumiere; in 1914 he was arrested and sent to southern Germany, was a prisoner of war; after returning to Estonia took part in founding the "Pallas" art school; People's Artist of the ESSR 1964.
