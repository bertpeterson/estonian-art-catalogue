Otto Paju

1926–2016 · Estonian printmaker (1926-2016)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid; Virumaa muuseumid

Fled through Finland to Sweden, graduated in 1947 from the Sigtuna Estonian Gymnasium and in 1951 from the Stockholm Higher School of Applied Arts. In the field of graphic art, from 1954-1955 he continued his studies in Oslo in the graphic art class of the Norwegian National Academy of Art under Chrix Dahl.
