August Jansen

1881–1957 · Estonian painter (1881-1957)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid

trained at Imperial Academy of Arts

Voldemar Vaga has stated in his "Eesti kunst" (1940) that although Jansen is among those artists who "tirelessly work on the comprehensive development of their artistic personality and abilities," and although his "development has continually been on an upward path," he "created his most mature and interesting works" precisely in the second half of the 1930s – Vaga considers especially good, in terms of the density and persuasiveness of artistic form, his fresh and cheerful-looking landscapes.
