Jaan Siirak

1897–1959 · Estonian artist (1897-1959)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

From 1932 he lived in Tallinn and from 1936 worked in the field of furniture industry and applied art. He was a member of EKKKÜ from 1935 and of Ra Kü from 1934. His work in the fine arts remained scant; he is better known as a furniture designer and interior designer. He promoted functionalism and applied art deco.
