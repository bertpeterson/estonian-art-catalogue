Heikki Leis

1973–
Allee galerii; NOBA

Heikki Leis (b. 1973) is a Tartu artist known above all for his hyperrealistic pencil drawings of famous Estonians. He graduated from the Tartu Art School in 1991 as a sculptor, and has worked as a freelancer since 2000. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
