Herbert Lukk

1892–1919 · Estonian painter (1892-1919)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Vaal galerii; Viljandi Muuseum

trained at Academy of Fine Arts of Helsinki · trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa · trained at Gustav Adolf Grammar School

H. Lukk studied art probably in 1912-1915 in Helsinki, at the school of the Finnish Art Society. He practised Art Nouveau and Neo-Impressionist art (influence of A. Finch). Voldemar Vaga called H. Lukk the only pure Neo-Impressionist in Estonian art. He participated in the survey exhibition of Estonian art in 1919, attracting attention.
