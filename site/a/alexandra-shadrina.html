Alexandra Shadrina

1985–
NOBA

Alexandra Shadrina, born in 1985 in South Kazakhstan, is a multidisciplinary artist and film director. She studied painting at the Moscow State Academic Art College named after 1905, animation at the ŠAR school, and furthered her education at the Baltic Film and Media School in Tallinn.
