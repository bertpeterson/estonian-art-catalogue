Ants Erik Vomm

1931–2003 · Estonian caricaturist (1931-2003)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

Born in Tartu as the son of the artist couple Benita and August Vomm. Lived in Rakvere as a child. From 1936 in Tallinn. In 1944 fled abroad with his mother and sister. Lived in Venezuela, Caracas, and from 1953 in Toronto, Canada. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
