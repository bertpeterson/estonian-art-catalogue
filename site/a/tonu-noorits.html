Tõnu Noorits

1952–
Artrovert; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Tõnu Noorits, PhD (b. 1952 in Tartu) graduated from the University of Tartu in 1975 as a biologist. He defended his doctoral degree in 1984. He has worked in photographic art since 1982. In 1992, together with Silver Vahter, he founded the design and art society KYNA, where he engages in photo-staging, design and publishing. He is a versatile creator, and his works – portraits, nudes, staged photographs, abstract landscapes – often contain unexpected visions. His photographs are characterised by a high pictorial culture and perfect execution. In the final decades of the last century he was the most exhibited Estonian author in European photo salons. He has had 35 solo exhibitions in various countries – Austria, Belgium, Italy, Latvia, France, Sweden, Germany, as well as 12 group exhibitions.
