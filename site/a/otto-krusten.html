Otto Krusten

1888–1937 · Estonian caricaturist (1888-1937)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa

Artist, caricaturist. Studied at Ranna rural school, at the Nikolai I Gymnasium in Tallinn, at A. Laikmaa's studio school and in St. Petersburg at the school of the Society for the Advancement of the Arts. Krusten's caricatures and cartoons appeared in the publications Sädemed, Kratt, Meie Mats, Kilk and others. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
