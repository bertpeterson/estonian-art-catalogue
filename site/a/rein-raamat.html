Rein Raamat (b. 1931) is an Estonian animated film director, artist and screenwriter. He is the first internationally successful Estonian animator, and together with Elbert Tuganov, Rein Raamat is often referred to as the father of Estonian animation. Raamat began his work in animated film and the art of animation as early as the early 1970s; he is also the author of more than 20 documentary films.
