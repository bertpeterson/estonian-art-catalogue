Andrei Jegorov

1878–1954 · Estonian artist (1878–1954)

Andrei Jegorov lived in St. Petersburg from 1887 to 1921. Having lost his hearing in childhood, he studied from 1887 to 1896 at the St. Petersburg school for the deaf and mute. In 1898 he entered the drawing school of the St. Petersburg Society for the Encouragement of the Arts. From 1901 to 1909 he studied as an auditor at the St. Petersburg Academy of Arts under Ilya Repin. He furthered his training in Munich at Dmitri Kardovski's studio. He then began working as a freelance artist. In 1919–1920 he was an artist with Semyon Budyonny's 1st Cavalry Army in the tsarist army. Having opted for Estonian citizenship, he worked as a freelance artist in Tallinn. During the Second World War he was evacuated to the rear of the USSR.
