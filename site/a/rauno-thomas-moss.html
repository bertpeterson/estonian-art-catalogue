Rauno Thomas Moss

1977– · Estonian artist (born 1977)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Rauno Thomas Moss (b. 1977 in Tallinn) is an Estonian painter and graphic designer who achieved widespread renown for his black-and-white colouring and autoerotic paintings. Carried by an interest in 19th-century medicine, Moss delves deeply into the touchpoints of his childhood memories and experiences, doing so in a vivid and slightly dreamlike surrealist estrangement. The result is disturbing and memorable. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
