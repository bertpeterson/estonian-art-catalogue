Ivan Zubaka

1960–
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Ivan Zubaka was born in Western Ukraine and has lived and worked in Estonia since 1982. He has been a member of the Estonian Sculptors' Association since 1993 and a member of the Estonian Artists' Association since 1997. Zubaka has created several public space works, including the gravestone of Igor Severyanin, the "Men of Vanishing" ("Kaduviku mehed") near the Rakvere Theatre, and carried out the restoration of the Tondipoisid Memorial. Zubaka has received several awards, including art prizes from the cities of Tallinn and Rakvere and the Viljandi city medal of merit. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
