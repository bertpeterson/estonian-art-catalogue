Tõnis Paberit

1952–
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Tõnis Paberit graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute in sculpture. He has worked as a lecturer in the art department of the University of Tartu, in the Konrad Mägi painting studio, at the Tartu Art School, and is currently an associate professor at the Pallas University of Applied Sciences. His main directions in creative work are psychological portraiture, conceptual installation and monumental art, as well as retrospection. Over the last decade Paberit has been working with peat and soil in sculpture. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
