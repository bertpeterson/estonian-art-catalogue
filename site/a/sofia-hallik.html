Sofia Hallik


NOBA

Sofia Hallik, a Tallinn-based artist, holds positions as a PhD researcher at the Estonian Academy of Arts and as the Creative Director of jewellery brands SOMA and ONEHE. Renowned for exhibiting her works locally and worldwide, Sofia has garnered recognition through numerous awards. Her work encapsulates the evolving narrative of art in the 21st century, where the tangible and the digital blend seamlessly. By exploring this intersection, Sofia aims to challenge perceptions, evoke emotions, and invite viewers into a world where tradition and technology coexist in harmony. In her artistic practice, Sofia Hallik blends traditional handwork with digital technology. Through mediums such as jewellery and paintings, she explores the synergy between artists&#8217; creativity and machine. In her jewellery, Sofia Hallik utilises digital technologies to create pieces that reflect the idea of a Future Renaissance. Each creation is a testament to the blend of traditional techniques with the limitless possibilities of the machine.
