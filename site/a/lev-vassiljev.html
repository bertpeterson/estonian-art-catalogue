Lev Vassiljev

1929–2020
Eesti Meremuuseum; NOBA; Narva Muuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

Painter, graphic artist, ex libris artist and poster artist Lev Vassiljev was born in Tula, Russia, into an engineer's family. After studying at the Moscow Art School he moved in 1950 to Tallinn, where he studied sculpture at ERKI (EKA), his teachers being Enn Roos, Aleksander Kaasik and Martin Saks. He also worked as a graphic artist (etching and aquatint), made collages and assemblages. Among his works there are pieces of political tone, while at the same time the artist was fond of mythology and literary themes. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
