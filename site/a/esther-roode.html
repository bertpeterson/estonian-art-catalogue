Esther ROODE (1923, Tallinn – 2007, Tallinn) is an Estonian painter who has created mainly portraits and landscapes in a nature-close manner. Roode obtained her art education in fashion at the Tallinn Women's Vocational School and the Tallinn State Institute of Applied Art, and in painting at the Tartu State Art Institute. Later she worked as a teacher of painting and drawing both at the Tallinn Pedagogical Institute and at ERKI. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
