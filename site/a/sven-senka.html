Sven Senka

1965– · Estonian artist (born 1965)
NOBA

Sven Senka is an Estonian artist who works mainly in the media of painting, sculpture and graphic art. His work moves in the borderlands between expressive and geometric abstraction, addressing philosophical, historical and existential themes. Senka's artistic practice is characterised by a subjective and idealistic approach, in which image, structure and colour function as means of interpretation.
