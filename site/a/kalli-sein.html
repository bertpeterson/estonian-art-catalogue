Kalli Sein


Hiiumaa Muuseumid

For a glass artist, Hiiumaa and Hüti therefore hold a special place. This prompted my wish to further introduce the art of glass in Hiiumaa, to tell the exciting story of glass's origin and development, and in my studio's work I have devoted a special place to objects connected with Hiiumaa."
