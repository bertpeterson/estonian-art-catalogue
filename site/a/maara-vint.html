Maara Vint

1955– · Estonian printmaker (born 1955)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Maara Vint was born on 31 October 1955 in Tallinn, Nõmme, into the family of the scientist Endel Vint. The sister of the artists Tõnis and Toomas Vint has inherited her love of flowers from her mother Tamara. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
