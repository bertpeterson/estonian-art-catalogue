Timur Hussainov


NOBA

Timur Husainov is an artist from Narva who combines various art forms in his work, including traditional painting, graphic design, and digital art. He is an active participant in art life and has taken part in local exhibitions, such as those organised by the "Vesterwalli" association. In addition, his work can be seen on the streets of Narva in the form of graffiti, which bring freshness and creativity to the city. Husainov draws inspiration from the urban environment and architecture, bringing a unique dynamism and vitality to his works. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
