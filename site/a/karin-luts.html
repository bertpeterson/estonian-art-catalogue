Karin Luts

1904–1993 · Estonian painter (1904-1993)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Pallas

Physician, studied in Berlin at Albert Reimann's art school in 1922, graduate of the University of Tartu (medicine 1923-1929), courses (sculpture) at the "Pallas" art school, private physician in Tallinn in the 1930s, evacuated to Tatarstan in 1941, physician in the Red Army from 1943, resident of the therapy department of Tallinn 2nd United Hospital from 1945, head, deputy chief physician for polyclinic matters, physician of the "Estonia" theatre, lecturer at the art institute in Tallinn.
