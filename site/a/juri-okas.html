Jüri Okas

1950– · Estonian architect (born 1950)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Studied construction at TPI (TTÜ, TalTech) 1968-1970 and from 1970 architecture at ERKI (EKA). Has created performances, installations, print series, short films etc. In graphic art he often uses photographs and film frames. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
