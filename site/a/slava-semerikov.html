Slava Semerikov


Haus Galerii; NOBA

Vjatšeslav Semerikov was born in Russia in 1949. He graduated in 1977 from the Estonian SSR State Art Institute in the speciality of graphic art. His main fields of creative work are free and applied graphic art, book design and painting. He has participated in numerous joint exhibitions since 1988 and has also organised solo exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad. His works are in galleries and private collections in Estonia, Russia, Finland, France, Italy, Greece, the United States of America, Japan, Sweden and the Netherlands. Vjatšeslav Semerikov is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and of the Association of Russian Artists operating in Estonia. Member of the Estonian Artists' Association.
