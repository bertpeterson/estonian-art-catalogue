Mati Karmin

1959– · Estonian sculptor (born 1959)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Eesti Spordi- ja Olümpiamuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid

Sculptor. He has created monumental works, portraits and figural compositions on mythological subjects. He has achieved expressiveness and plastic richness of expression by combining various classical materials (plaster, bronze, stone, wood) with one another, or using them separately or together with ready-made materials. Characteristic of him is the interweaving of the ironic and the grotesque with the serious. Since the 1990s he has created installations and conceptual projects.
