Belliisi Seenemaa


NOBA

Belliisi Seenemaa is an artist and art teacher. Belliisi has studied at the Tartu Art School in decorator-stylist specialty and at Tartu Art College Pallas – textile specialty. She has furthered her education in Berlin in the field of combining e-textiles and technologies, studied archaic leather art techniques in France, and traditional methods of processing plant fibres and dyes in Estonia. In previous years her interests have centred on symbolism, ritual themes and objects. In recent years, however, her work has focused primarily on closeness to nature, nostalgia and experimentation with pigments and minerals.
