Mati Kütt

1947– · Estonian film director and caricaturist (born 1947)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

Mati Kütt (b. 1947 in Tallinn) is a painter and animated film director. In 2001–2002 he worked in America, in Denver, the capital of the state of Colorado, as a lecturer in experimental animation at the Rocky Mountain College of Art and Design. Kütt is a member of the Estonian Filmmakers' Union (1985), the Estonian Artists' Association (1999) and the Estonian Painters' Association (1999).
