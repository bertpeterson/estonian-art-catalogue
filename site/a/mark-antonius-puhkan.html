Mark Antonius Puhkan

1994–
NOBA; Vaal galerii

Mark Antonius Puhkan (b. 1994) is an Estonian freelance artist, graphic artist, illustrator and lecturer. He has obtained a Bachelor's degree in Graphic Art and a Master's degree in Contemporary Art from the Estonian Academy of Arts. In his free artistic practice he has exhibited in galleries mainly in the mediums of drawing and printmaking, both in Estonia and abroad. As an illustrator he is active in the fields of fiction, journalism and scientific illustration alike. He is a lecturer of graphic art and drawing subjects at the Estonian Academy of Arts. In his artistic position, an important place is held by the observation of being human and the recreation of physical and mental bridges between man and wild nature.
