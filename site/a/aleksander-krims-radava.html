Aleksander Krims (Radava)

1893–1947 · Estonian painter (1893-1947)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa

Studied 1913-1917 at Ants Laikmaa's studio school in Tallinn, 1917-1918 belonged to the Young Artists' Union "Vikerla". Belonged to the Estonian Artists' Group in the late 1920s. In 1928 was in Paris, in 1931 exhibited in Helsinki, at the annual exhibition of the Finnish Artists' Union. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
