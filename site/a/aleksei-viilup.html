Aleksei Viilup

1916–1978 · Estonian artist (1916-1978)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Graduated from Valga Joint Gymnasium in 1935 and from the J. Koort State Applied Art School in 1941. Worked in Tallinn during World War II and in 1965–78 as a design artist in the decoration studio of Ars, together with V. Tomassov. Member of EKL from 1939, member of KL from 1946. Designed exhibitions and major events (incl. the 12th General Song Festival in 1947 and, in the 1950s, Tallinn song days and processions). Practised portrait and decorative painting as well as poster art. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
