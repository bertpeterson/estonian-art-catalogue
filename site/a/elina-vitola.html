Elīna Vītola

1986–
Kogo galerii; NOBA

Elina Vitola (b. 1986) is a young Latvian artist who introduces herself as follows: "I have been joyfully learning to paint for almost my entire life, and in 2018 I will complete my master's studies at the Art Academy of Latvia. The exhibitions I have had the greatest pleasure of taking part in are, for example, "Children of the New East" at the Tallinn Art Hall and "Viewing and Reading Room" at Kogo Gallery in Tartu. Recently I have made several abstract paintings, but in the near future I am planning to paint pasta." (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
