Lydia Nirk-Soosaar

1908–1995 · Estonian sculptor (1908-1995)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage; Viljandi Muuseum

trained at Pallas

Graduated in 1926 from the Viljandi Estonian Education Society Girls' Gymnasium. At the beginning of 1928 he entered the Higher Art School Pallas, where he studied painting initially in Nikolai Triik's studio, then in Ado Vabbe's studio. He graduated from the school in 1936 together with Johannes Võerahansu, Richard Sagrits, Karl Pärsimägi and Ellinor Aikas. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
