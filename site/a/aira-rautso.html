Aira Rautso

1954– · Estonian artist (born 1954)
NOBA; Narva Muuseum

Aira Rautso (b. 1954) is an Estonian painter and art teacher. She graduated from Tallinn University in 1978 and has worked since 1980 as an art teacher at the Jõhvi Art School. Aira Rautso has been a member of the Estonian Painters' Association since 2008. She has participated in numerous solo and group exhibitions in Estonia and abroad, including in Scandinavia and Germany. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
