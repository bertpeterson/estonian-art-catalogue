Stanislav Netšvolodov

1935–
NOBA; Tartu Linnamuuseum

Stanislav Netšvolodov (b. 1935 in Ukraine) is an Estonian sculptor, medallist and painter. He graduated from the Kiev Institute of Civil Engineers with a degree in architecture. Netšvolodov has created bronze, wood and stone sculptures and monuments and also works in painting and graphic art. Netšvolodov's favourite pursuit is medal art; he is the author of several medals of the University of Tartu (including the university's honorary doctorate medal and the Skytte Medal). Netšvolodov has been active in sculpture since 1962. In 2002 Stanislav Netšvolodov was awarded the Anton Starkopf Art Prize. He is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and F.I.D.E.M.
