Ivan Arhipovitš Bersenev

1762–1789
Pärnu Muuseum

His work was noticed by Napoleon, and as a result in 1794 he became the head of a group of experts who were to select works of art in the Spanish Netherlands (now Belgium) to be sent to Paris. He carried out the same tasks in Italy as well. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
