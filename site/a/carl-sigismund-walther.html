Carl Sigismund Walther

1783–1866 · Baltic German sculptor (1783-1866)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Born in Dresden as the son of a craftsman, studied at the Dresden Academy of Arts. The writer August von Kotzebue invited him to Estonia as a home tutor for his children; Walther worked at Vardi manor and at the Hageri parsonage in the service of Provost Ignatius, who was a relative of Kotzebue. In 1812 Walther moved to Tallinn and in 1815 became the art teacher at Tallinn Cathedral School. In Tallinn he was the first to work as a lithographer, painted portraits and paintings for church altarpieces. In 1835 Walther received the title of free artist from the Saint Petersburg Academy of Arts. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
