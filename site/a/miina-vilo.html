Miina Vilo

1995–
NOBA

Miina Vilo (b. 1995) is an Estonian artist working mainly in Tallinn and Tartu. She obtained a bachelor's degree in painting from the Pallas University of Applied Sciences (2025) and is a member of the art group MULTINEKTAR (founded 2024). Vilo's work has been described as an emotional and versatile synthesis of pop art, surrealism and symbolist elements. In 2023/24 she received the D'Albret Collection: The Reiss von Filski Trust prize. The jury highlighted her ability to tell stories through symbols that invite the viewer on a journey of dreams and reflections, noting that the artist's individuality is palpably present in every work. Vilo has actively participated in exhibitions and art auctions. More information and the full CV can be found on the artist's website: www.miinavilo.com/cv.
