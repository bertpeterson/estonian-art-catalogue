Andres Adamson's reply: "Sirje is my mother, who today (28.09.2021) happens to turn 81, and Võidu 18-8 was our address in Kärdla, 1963?-1972. My mother is also currently in Kassari, I'm only going to the birthday afterwards, on Sunday, I'll show her this picture and ask whose it is. Because of the (lack of) level, I assume it's hers. My mother indeed had a proper artistic talent too, she was also supposed to go to ERKI, but by the time they married my father was already there, and they lived in such cramped conditions that two family members studying at the same time was difficult. It was postponed and in the end never happened. I doubt my father's authorship because when was Rannapaargu finished?, probably 1966, he was already 28 then, schools behind him, I wouldn't want to believe such a poor technical level, he later won medals at the Baltic Watercolour Biennials etc. Also the signature is not written in his hand (though it doesn't resemble my mother's either), and he would never under any circumstances have written his own address next to a picture. Unfortunately I can no longer ask my father." (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
