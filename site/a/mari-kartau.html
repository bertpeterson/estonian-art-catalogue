Mari Kartau

1968– · Estonian artist, art historian, art critic and curator (born 1968)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

member of Estonian Artists' Association · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Mari Kartau aka Siram (b. 1968) has graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in art history, studied at the non-formal art education institution Academia Non Grata, and also holds a forestry education. In her work she has mainly engaged in performance and painting, and to a lesser extent has also made installations and graphic art. She has curated numerous exhibitions and written art criticism. She was more active in the field of art in 1992–2015, but is still active today. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
