Fred Kotkas

1986–
NOBA; Temnikova & Kasela

Fred Kotkas (1986) is a painter and illustrator who graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts, whose introspective work focuses on the inner world of the human being and the factors shaping it. His mode of expression is vivid and figurative, accompanied by a self-ironic attitude, which adds a certain playfulness to his works. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
