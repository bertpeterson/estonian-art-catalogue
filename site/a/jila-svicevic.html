Jila Svicevic

1991–
NOBA

Jila Svicevic (born 1991) is an intermedia artist. He was born in Novi Sad, Yugoslavia, but currently lives and works in Tallinn, Estonia. He has studied art in Estonian Academy of Arts (New Media, Design), University of Nova Gorcia (Media Arts and Practises; MA) and Hungarian University of Fine Arts (Master of Fine Arts in Intermedia). He has had many exhibitions in Hungary and Russia. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
