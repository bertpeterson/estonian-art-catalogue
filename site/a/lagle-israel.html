Lagle Israel

1923–2000 · Estonian artist (1923-2000)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Tartu Art School

Worked as a freelance artist in Tartu. Member of the Estonian Artists' Union (EKL) from 1950. Created sculptural portraits, practised monumental art, made flower paintings in pastel technique. In graphic art, preferred colour monotype and aquatint. Exhibitions from 1951. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
