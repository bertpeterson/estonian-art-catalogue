Lagle Israel

1923–2000 · Estonian artist (1923-2000)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Tartu Art School

He studied sculpture from 1942 at the Tallinn School of Applied Art, studied from 1943 at Pallas and from 1944 to 1950 at the Tartu State Art Institute (in A. Starkopf's class). He has worked as a freelance artist in Tartu. Member of the Artists' Union from 1950. He has created sculptural portraits, practised monumental art, made flower paintings in pastel technique. In graphic art he has preferred coloured monotype and aquatint. Exhibiting from 1951. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
