Tõnis Laanemaa

1937–2024 · Estonian graphic designer (1937-2024)

member of ANK '64 · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Tõnis Laanemaa (born 1937) studied painting at the Tartu Art School and graphic art at the Estonian State Institute. Initial leader and later member of ANK'64, the innovative Estonian art group of the 1960s. Tõnis began exhibiting with paintings, but gained recognition primarily for his free graphic art and posters, although throughout his career he has also exhibited nudes, portraits and abstract compositions. Since 1970 Laanemaa has taken part in numerous exhibitions in the Baltic states, Poland, Hungary, Czechia, Germany, Russia, and the Netherlands. Tõnis Laanemaa belongs to the generation of artists for whom proper drawing from nature is self-evident. He has also gained recognition as a figure drawer and is a highly regarded drawing teacher.
