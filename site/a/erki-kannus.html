Erki Kannus


NOBA

Erki Kannus graduated from ERKI in 1981 in the field of glass art. He has been exhibiting since 1976. He has created several works of monumental art for public spaces. From 1989 to 2000 he resided in Germany, the USA and Sweden. Since 2000 he has actively participated in Estonian art, both in solo and group exhibitions, as well as exhibitions in Latvia, Lithuania, Sweden, Poland, Ukraine, France and Bulgaria. In his work he combines various techniques (painting, printmaking, sculpture) and materials (glass, wood, metal) with sound and video. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
