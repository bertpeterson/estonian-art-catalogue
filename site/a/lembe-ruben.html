Lembe Ruben

1974– · Estonian printmaker (born 1974)
Artrovert; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

Lembe Ruben (b. 1974) graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in the field of graphic art. Master's degree in fine arts 2002. Wiiralt Young Artist Prize 2011. She has taught graphic art, drawing, and composition at the Estonian Academy of Arts and in hobby groups; designed and illustrated magazines and books; and has also engaged in animation, stage design, and exhibition curating.
