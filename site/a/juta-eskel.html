Juta Eskel

1927–2017 · Estonian sculptor (1927-2017)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Tallinn into an engineer's family. In 1948 she graduated, together with her future husband Albert Eskel and several other well-known artists of her generation, from the Tallinn State Institute of Applied Art, but was not given a diploma for political reasons. Her father Raiu Mullari was a prominent Estonian War of Independence veteran, and her brother Rünno Mullari, a talented mathematician, was sent to a prison camp by the occupying authorities. In 1948-1952 Juta Eskel worked as a drawing teacher in several schools in Tallinn, in 1952-1957 she found work as an artist at the Art Products Combine.
