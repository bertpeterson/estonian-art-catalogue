Rebeka Vaino

1995–
NOBA

Rebeka Vaino (b. 1995, Tallinn, Estonia) is an artist working between London and Paris, known for her colourful and conceptual works. She recently completed her Master of Fine Arts (MFA) at Goldsmiths, University of London (January 2025). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
