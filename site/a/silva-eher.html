Silva Eher

1954–2021 · Estonian artist (1954-2021)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

From 1972 to 1977 he worked as an artist-copyist in the animated film group of Eesti Telefilm. In 1977 he began studies at ERKI, graduating in 1983 as a painter-pedagogue. He began participating in exhibitions in 1982. From 1983 to 1984 he worked as an artist-painter at Tallinnfilm's animated film studio. From 1984 to 1989 he was a teacher of artistic special subjects at Tallinna Kergetööstustehnikum. Member of the Estonian Artists' Association since 1989. Works are held in the collections of the Art Museum of Estonia, the Tartu Art Museum, the Ministry of Culture of Russia, the Tretyakov Gallery, the Alma-Ata Art Museum and the Uzhgorod Art Museum in Kazakhstan, as well as in private collections.
