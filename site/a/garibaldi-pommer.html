Garibaldi Pommer

1908–1972 · Estonian sculptor (1908-1972)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

On 21 June 1940 he was the leader of the June coup in Tartu. In July 1940 he was the responsible editor of the newspaper Kommunist, and from 8 July to August 1940 he was the chief political leader of the army. This last post was established by decision of President Konstantin Päts of 5 July 1940. Paul Keerdo was the first and last person to hold this post.
