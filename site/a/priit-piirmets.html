Priit Piirmets


Virumaa Muuseumid

Priit Piirmets was born on 15.03.1948 in Hageri Parish. He came to Rakvere in 1956. He studied at Rakvere 1st 8-year School. He graduated from the Rakvere Correspondence Secondary School. At Rakvere 1st 8-year School, drawing was taught by teacher Gustavson. During his secondary school years he attended the art circle at the House of Culture, led by Ella Rajand. From 1973 he was himself the leader of an art circle. He was a member of the Artists' Union Youth Group. He considers Luulik Kokamägi to be his teacher and role model. In 1974 he moved to live in Tallinn. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
