Liisamari Viik


NOBA

Liisamari Viik is a video and performance artist. She has completed master's studies in scenography at the Estonian Academy of Arts and furthered her studies in video at the Vienna Academy of Fine Arts (2025). Viik has received the Young Artist Prize (2026), the Eduard Wiiralt Scholarship (2024) and the Helju Rossmann-Schneider Scholarship (2025). As an artist she has worked on various projects in exhibitions, theatre and film. Recently Liisamari has mainly been engaged in exploring, rhythmising and practising inhuman precision.
