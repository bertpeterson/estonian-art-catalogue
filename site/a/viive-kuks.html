Viive Kuks

1951– · Estonian graphic artist (born 1951)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Viive Kuks began her art studies in 1966 at the Tallinn Light Industry Technical School in the field of artistic textile design, which she completed in 1970. That same year Kuks began studies at the Tallinn Art University (EKA) in the field of graphic art. Her main teacher was Evald Okas. In 1976 she received a diploma in printmaking and moved to live in Võru, working as a freelance artist. Viive Kuks has been a member of the Estonian Artists' Association since 1984, and has participated in exhibitions since 1977. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
