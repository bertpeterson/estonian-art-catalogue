Marju Mutsu

1941–1980 · Estonian graphic designer (1941-1980)
Allee galerii; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum

member of ANK '64 · member of Estonian Artists' Association · trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Tallinn, studied at the 7th Secondary School and in 1962-1969 graphic art at ERKI. Was a founding member of the ANK 64 group. Worked in the editorial office of the magazine "Noorus" in 1961-1962. Published articles, wrote aphorisms and poems. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
