Peter Paul Rubens

1577–1640 · Flemish painter (1577–1640)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum

member of Antwerp Guild of Saint Luke · movement Baroque · movement Flemish Baroque painting

During the eight years spent in Italy, the artist developed his distinctive style. Rubens's versatility as an artist became apparent: altarpieces, portraits, paintings with mythological and allegorical content, and religious compositions. The young Rubens's works were of superb technical execution. By around 1620 his painting style had fully developed. Its characteristics were figures of lush, vigorous forms, passionate movements and brilliant colours. He is characterised by the depiction of plump nude women.
