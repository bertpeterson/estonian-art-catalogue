Andrei Maksimjuk


Artrovert; Haus Galerii; NOBA

Andrei Maksimjuk is an Estonian artist. He was born in Tallinn and has lived for years in Milan. He obtained his bachelor's and master's degrees in painting and drawing at the Estonian Academy of Arts and the Brera Academy of Fine Arts in Milan – institutions that have played a key role in the artist's career. Maksimjuk has worked in various fields: restoration, teaching, exhibition planning, design and, above all, painting. He has also experimented with various techniques: drawing, collage and mixed media. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
