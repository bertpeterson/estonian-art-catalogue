Eero Ijavoinen

1992–
NOBA

Eero Ijavoinen graduated from Tallinn Pedagogical University as an art teacher (1992) and has taught at Paide Ühisgümnaasium and at Tartu Higher Art School. Ijavoinen paints and makes figures. His paintings resemble abstractions derived from landscapes, and his sculptures are made from wood and found objects. Ijavoinen has been exhibiting since 1988.
