Marje Taska

1955– · Estonian artist (born 1955)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

Marje Taska was born in 1955 in Tallinn, graduated from the Tallinn Music High School in piano, and studied German philology at the University of Tartu. She began her art studies with Tõnis Vint, being his first student. Her further art and language studies belong already to her period of life in Sweden. After moving to Stockholm in 1982, she has lived both in Lapland and in southern Sweden. Today her home city is once again Stockholm, where, in parallel with Tallinn, she lives and works as a freelance artist and curator. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
