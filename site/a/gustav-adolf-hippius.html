Gustav Adolf Hippius

1792–1856 · Baltic German painter and lithographer (1792-1856)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Studied painting in Vienna and Rome. From 1820 worked in Petersburg and from 1849 in Tallinn as a portraitist and drawing teacher. Also created portraits of Estonians and 8 volumes of lithographic portraits of Russian statesmen, scholars and artists ("Les contemporains", from 1822, Russian-language edition 1828) in the spirit of early realism, i.e. Biedermeier realism (collection of articles "Four Baltic German Artists", Tallinn 1994, exhibition catalogue "Russian Rulers", Tallinn 2006) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
