Nigul Espe

1907–1970 · Estonian artist (1907-1970)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Pallas

He studied painting and furniture art at the Pallas Higher Art School (1926–1932). After graduating from Pallas he worked as a freelance artist in Tartu, where between 1934 and 1937 he ran the legendary café for cultural figures, "Ko-Ko-Ko". After the war, between 1945 and 1947, he worked as a drawing teacher at Tartu 1st Secondary School, and from 1947 to 1950 as chairman of the Tartu bureau of the Art Fund. Later he made illustrations for ERM publications and magazines and created caricatures. He was a member of the Central Union of Estonian Visual Artists from 1932 and a member of the Estonian SSR Artists' Union from 1945. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
