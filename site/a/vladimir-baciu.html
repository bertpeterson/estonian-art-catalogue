Vladimir Baciu

1965–
Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vernissage

Vladimir Baciu (b. 1965 in Gilicenis, Moldova) graduated as a painter-pedagogue from the I. Repin Art School in Kishinev (1984). Since 1985 he has lived and worked in Tallinn. He is a member of the Estonian Artists' Union and of the Estonian-Russian artists' group OKO. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
