Marion Saarik


NOBA; Vernissage

Marion Saarik graduated in painting from the Estonian Academy of Arts. In her work she explores the complex aspects of time, perception and self-expression through a monochrome pictorial language. Monochrome figures reveal to the viewer the movement between reality and perception, as well as the artist's inner struggles. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
