Paul Luhtein

1909–2007 · Estonian artist, printmaker and pedagogue (1909-2007)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Viljandi Muuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Studied at the State School of Applied Arts in the years 1924-1930. Furthered his studies in the years 1931-1932 in the master class of the Leipzig State Academy for Graphic Arts and Book Design. He then taught for a long time at the State Industrial School. In 1944 he obtained a teaching position at the Estonian SSR State Institute of Applied Arts, where he became head of the graphic art department (docent 1946, professor 1952) and remained in this position until 1982.
