Enn Roos

1908–1990 · Estonian artist (1908–1990)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Narva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Pallas

In 1939 member of the art association Pallas. In 1941 he was a lecturer at the J. Koort State School of Applied Art and was subsequently mobilised into the Soviet army. In 1942-44 he worked in Moscow as a sculptor. In 1944-46 lecturer at the Tallinn State Art Institute, in 1947-83 at ERKI (from 1958 professor, 1962-77 head of the sculpture department) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
