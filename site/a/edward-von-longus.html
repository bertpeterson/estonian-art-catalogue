Edward von Lõngus

Estonian street artist with undisclosed real name
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

According to his own words, Edward von Lõngus does not physically exist. There is only an idea. He is a jointly shared theoretical concept, a reflection of the era of multi-identity in the information field, generated from the vibrations of the collective consciousness. He was conceived in the fertile conditions of the information society through the coincidence of many different thoughts, and was born between the lines as a side effect of the 21st century. He is a collection of thoughts in a vast information field, spreading from consciousness to consciousness like a non-computer computer virus. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
