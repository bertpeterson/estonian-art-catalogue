Aleksei Shatunov

1981–
Haus Galerii; NOBA

Aleksei Shatunov (b. 1981) graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts with a master's degree in art pedagogy (2009); in 2008 he furthered his studies in Spain at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia, Facultad de Bellas Artes. He is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Union of Russian Artists in Estonia. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
