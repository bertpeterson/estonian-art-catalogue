Aleksander Tassa

1882–1955 · Estonian author, painter and writer (1882–1955)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Born in Tartu. Studied in 1897-1898 at the drawing courses of the Tartu German Craftsmen's Society. In 1904 at Baron A. Stieglitz's School of Applied Art in Petersburg, in 1905-1906 at Ants Laikmaa's studio in Tallinn and in 1906 at J. Goldblatt's art courses in Petersburg. In 1906 he moved to Helsinki, in 1907 to Paris, where he continued his art studies. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
