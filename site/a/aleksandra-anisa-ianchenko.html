Aleksandra ANISA Ianchenko

1988–
NOBA

Aleksandra ANISA Ianchenko (born 1988) is a visual artist, university researcher and exhibition curator. An artist with a background in fine arts and art history, she recently defended her doctoral degree at Tallinn University (Estonia) and Åbo Akademi (Finland) with the dissertation "Estranging Trams: Atmospheres of Trams in and through Art", which is based on her artistic practice. In her work she is interested in creating imagined worlds rooted in everyday observations. These worlds can take various forms — from paintings and drawings to performative interventions and filmmaking. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
