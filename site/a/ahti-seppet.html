Ahti Seppet

1953– · Estonian sculptor (born 1953)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Ahti Seppet (b. 1953 in Tartu) is mainly known as a sculptor and a master of sculptural installations, but has also practised painting and printmaking. Seppet graduated in 1973 from the Tartu Art School in the specialty of artistic wood design. He has participated in exhibitions since 1978 (as a painter). Since 1990 Seppet has been a member of the Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
