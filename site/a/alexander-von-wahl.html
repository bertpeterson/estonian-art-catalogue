Alexander von Wahl

1839–1903 · Baltic German artist (1839-1903)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Studied at the Saint Petersburg Academy of Arts 1858-1861. Teacher was sculptor Peter Clodt von Jürgensburg. Then went to Munich, where he studied under Widemann. Was forced for health reasons to give up sculpture and began to paint. Worked mainly in Munich. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
