Urmas Puhkan

1967– · Estonian ceramicist (born 1967)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Urmas Puhkan (1967) graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in ceramics in 1992. Already during his studies he participated in exhibitions and is a founder of the art group Neoeksprepost, active since 1988. He has created sculptural ceramics, curated ceramics exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad, and was one of the initiators of the ARS Ceramics Centre.
