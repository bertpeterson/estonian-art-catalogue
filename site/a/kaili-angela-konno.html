Kaili-Angela Konno

1970–
NOBA

Kaili-Angela Konno (b. 1970 in the town of Valga) has participated as a painter in numerous exhibitions since 1997. Konno holds a bachelor's degree in painting from the Estonian Academy of Arts (1999). "I have sought internal and external shifts in space and in the person. This lets things appear from an unusual viewpoint, which creates connections and convergences on a new plane, which ultimately balances into a whole," the artist herself says in explanation of her work, in an interview given to Vano Allsalu, the current president of the Estonian Artists' Association.
