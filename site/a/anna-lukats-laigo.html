Anna Lukats-Laigo

1899–1938 · Estonian artist (1900-1937)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Graduated from the Pallas Art School in 1929, worked as a drawing teacher at the Võru Joint Gymnasium, in 1937 moved to Tartu and worked as a teacher at the evening gymnasium. Exhibited at exhibitions of the Pallas Art Society and KKSKV, belonged to the Estonian Artists' Group. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
