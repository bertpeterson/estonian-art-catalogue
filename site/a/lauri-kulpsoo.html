Lauri Kulpsoo

1978– · Estonian photographer and musician (born 1978)
Ruki galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Lauri Kulpsoo is an Estonian photographer and drummer. He is a graduate of Tartu Tamme Gymnasium and has studied geography at the University of Tartu. Lauri Kulpsoo has worked as a photographer for the newspaper Postimees and the magazine Pere ja Kodu. He has also contributed to several other Estonian and Finnish media publications. Kulpsoo's photographs have been used in several books. Kulpsoo has played drums in the bands Lloyd, Tartu Popi ja Roki Instituut, Jaan Pärn ja Pahad Seemned, Honey Power and Kuma. Since autumn 2008 Lauri Kulpsoo has been making "Fantaasia" programmes on Klassikaraadio. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
