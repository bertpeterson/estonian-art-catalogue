Johannes Luik

1988–
Kogo galerii; NOBA; Tütar galerii

Johannes Luik (1988) was born in Tartu. In 2019 he obtained a bachelor's degree at the Estonian Academy of Arts in the Department of Installation and Sculpture. In 2021 he obtained a master's degree in Fine Arts at the LUCA School of Arts in Belgium. As a sculptor and installation artist, Luik is interested in the materiality of (objects) and the parameters conveyed through it that describe the properties of the physical world, such as material, texture, mass, volume and dimensionality. He is interested in the relationship of (abstract) objects to three-dimensional architectural space and the functional relationship of architectural details to these objects. Luik says: "This kind of intervention points to the potential of a space to look different or to be used differently. As an installation artist, space thus becomes a material which, when activated, makes it possible to create different ways of experiencing and remembering." In 2018 and 2019 he received the Young Sculptor Award, and in 2020 he was recognized with the Adamson-Eric scholarship. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
