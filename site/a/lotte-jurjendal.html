Lotte Jürjendal


NOBA

Lotte Jürjendal is an Estonian multidisciplinary artist whose work combines painting, sound art, textile, installation and sculpture. Selected solo exhibitions: 2022 – "Baroque in Metaverse", Solaris Gallery, Tallinn. The exhibition combined hand-embroidered haute couture, sound and textile painting. 2022 – "Avatars", OKAPI Gallery, Tallinn. An installation exhibition exploring virtual identity, cosmos and embodiment through painting, sound art, embroidered sculptures and light installations.
