Ernst Tiido

1913–1957 · Estonian printmaker (1913-1957)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum

trained at Pallas · trained at Tartu Art School

Entered Pallas in 1939, his teachers of printmaking were Arkadio Laigo and Ado Vabbe. In 1946 he graduated from the Tartu State Art Institute. From 1947-1949 he worked there as a printmaking instructor, after which he was a freelance artist. Member of the Artists' Union from 1946. He practised various intaglio printing techniques. He is best known as a master of cityscapes and landscape views. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
