Erna Kreischmann

1885–1929 · Baltic German painter (1885–1929)
Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa

Interior view of Pärnu St. Nicholas' Church towards the main nave, facing west. Opens onto the wall with low arcade arches separating the central nave from the southern side nave, with pilasters and windows above. At the end of the main nave the organ gallery with the organ, and the church's western entrance below it. On the right, a pulpit with a canopy. In the church, rows of pews with closed doors. Light falls from the windows of the south nave on the left, the church is generally dim. The sides of the triumphal arch frame the view from both sides.;.
