Andrus Johani

1906–1941 · Estonian painter (1906–1941)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts · trained at Pallas

In 1933, member of the art association Pallas. Travelled in the USSR in 1934 and 1935, in 1936-1937 furthered his education as a scholarship holder of the Manufacturers' Association in Berlin, Paris and Amsterdam. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
