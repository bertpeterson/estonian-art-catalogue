Asko Künnap

1971– · Estonian journalist and writer (born 1971)
NOBA

Studied at the Estonian Academy of Arts and the Oslo Academy of Arts. Creative director of the publishing house Näo Kirik and design studio AKT7. Recognised with awards in book design, marketing and advertising. Has published seven collections of poetry in Estonia and three in Finland. Cultural Endowment of Estonia annual literary award, the Estonian National Culture Foundation's debut prize and the Looming annual award. Has developed as an artist in printmaking, super-graphics, painting, photography, illustration, animation and comics. Solo exhibitions have taken place in Estonia, Finland, Sweden and Norway.
