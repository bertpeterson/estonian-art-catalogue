Oskar Hoffmann

1851–1912 · architect
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii

Born 05.02.1851, Tartu; died 03.03.1912. Education: Received basic knowledge in painting in Tartu from K. von Kügelgen and in lithography in C. A. Shulz's workshop. Continued studies 1872-77 at the Düsseldorf Academy of Art, including in 1874 with E. von Gebhardt and from 1875 with E. Dücker. Made study trips to Westphalia, the upper reaches of the Rhine and the North Sea islands. Studied briefly in Paris. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
