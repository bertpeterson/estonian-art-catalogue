Eerik Haamer

1908–1994 · Estonian painter (1908–1994)
Allee galerii; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

trained at Pallas · trained at University of Tartu

He fled on the night of 19.09.1944 together with 12 companions to Sweden, rowing a rescue boat stolen from the Germans. Afterwards he first visited Kuressaare in May 1986, then again in 1991. He also visited Estonia in 1970 and 1977. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
