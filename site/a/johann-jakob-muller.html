Johann Jakob Müller

1765–1832 · German painter and engraver (1765-1832)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Studied theology in Jena, Göttingen and Erlangen. In 1790 he entered the Dresden Academy of Art to study painting under academician Chr. Klengel. After repeated stays in Italy he settled in Stuttgart, where he became a teacher at the art school and royal court painter. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
