Paul Raud

1865–1930 · Estonian artist (1865-1930)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

In 1902, on the initiative of teacher Wieckmann and with the permission of the Consistory, the winged altarpiece (B. Notke, 1483), as a Catholic-era object unsuitable for a Lutheran church, was replaced with a new altar wall. In 1929 the parish council had agreed to donate the winged altarpiece to the Museum Society, but in connection with the church's renovation works in 1933, efforts began to have it returned. The Museum Society did not agree, despite expert assessments that considered the storage place in the Cathedral unsuitable for the altarpiece. Only on 18 December 1942 was the winged altarpiece reconsecrated in its own place. (source: National Register of Cultural Monuments);. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
