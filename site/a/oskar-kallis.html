Oskar Kallis

1892–1918 · Estonian painter (1892-1918)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa

Kallis began his art studies in 1907 at Ants Laikmaa's studio school, initially managing to study there for only one semester, but continued with Laikmaa in 1913-1916, meanwhile furthering his education in 1912-13 at the Drawing Courses of the Estonian Art Society.
