Voldemar Mölder

1904–1986 · Estonian scenographer and painter (1904-1986)
Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts · trained at Pallas

From Rakvere, studied at "Pallas" 1927-1936 and graduated as a student of N. Triik. He furthered his education a year later in Paris, Berlin and Dresden. From 1940-1965 he worked as a stage designer at the theatre of his home town Rakvere: he designed about 150 productions. He was a painter of good professional level, who also exhibited at national exhibitions. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
