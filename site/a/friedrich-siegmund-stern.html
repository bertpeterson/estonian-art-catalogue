Friedrich Siegmund Stern

1812–1889
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum

1856–1871 an album of Saaremaa folk costumes was published as separate sheets, Die Trachten d. XII Kirchspiele d. Oeselschen Kreises; it contained 14 sheets of lithographs. In a reworked form it was published as Album oeselscher Bauerntrachten (20 sheets); in 1914 a reprint (13 sheets) was published in Riga. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
