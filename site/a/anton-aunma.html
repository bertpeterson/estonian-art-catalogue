Anton Aunma

1972– · Estonian painter (born 1972)
E-Kunstisalong; NOBA

Anton Aunma was born in Tallinn in 1972. Anton Aunma graduated from the Tartu Art School in 1993. His graduation work was a series of views of Tartu. The artist has taken part in exhibitions since 1999. The artist mainly paints landscapes, cityscapes and the sea. To a lesser extent, Anton Aunma's oeuvre also includes nudes and still lifes. His principal technique used is oil painting. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
