Eetu Sihvonen

1994–
NOBA; Temnikova & Kasela

Eetu Sihvonen (born 1994) will receive his BFA in sculpture at the Academy of Fine Arts, Helsinki by the end of 2019. Previously Sihvonen has studied in Aalto University, department of graphic design. His works have previously been shown in several group exhibitions in Finland.
