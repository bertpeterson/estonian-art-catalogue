Raivo Kolka


Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Viljandi Muuseum

Graduated in 1937 from Viljandi County Boys' Gymnasium and in 1946 from the University of Tartu Faculty of Medicine. 1946–1951 assistant at the Department of General Surgery of the University of Tartu, 1951–1952 assistant at the Department of Hospital Surgery, 1952–1960 head of the surgery department of Jõhvi Hospital, 1960–1962 surgeon at Viljandi District Central Hospital, 1962–1968 head of the surgery department, 1968–1978 deputy chief physician for treatment, 1978–1994 surgeon at the hospital and polyclinic.
