Hanna Piksarv

1989–
NOBA

Hanna Piksarv (b. 1989) is a sculptor and visual artist, who obtained a master's degree in sculpture in 2015 at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Since 2009, Hanna Piksarv has participated in group exhibitions in Estonia and Austria; in recent years the main focus has been on collaborative projects and solo exhibitions. Among them the solo exhibition "Hea ja halg", dealing with the theme of body, time and work, at the Tartu Art House in 2014, and the project "Excellence in Ebensee", weighing the values of craft, within the framework of the Festival of Regions programme in Austria. A currently ongoing project dealing with the synergy of thinking and handiwork will likewise reach audiences in both Tallinn and Vienna. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
