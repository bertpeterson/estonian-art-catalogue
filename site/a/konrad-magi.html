Konrad Mägi

1878–1925 · Estonian artist (1878-1925)
Allee galerii; Asukoht teadmata; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahvusarhiiv

movement Expressionism · trained at Académie Colarossi

Studied 1888-1889 at the Uderna ministry school, then in Tartu at Pelberg's primary school and in 1891 at the town school. Received basic artistic knowledge 1899-1902 at the drawing courses of the Tartu German Craftsmen's Society. Continued art studies 1903-1905 as an auditor in St. Petersburg at Baron Alexander Stieglitz's School of Technical Drawing, focusing, as a student of Amandus Adamson, on wood carving and modelling.
