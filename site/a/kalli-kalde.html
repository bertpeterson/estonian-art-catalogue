Kalli Kalde

1967–
Artrovert; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Kalli Kalde (b. 1967) is a painter and printmaker who graduated from the Tartu Art School in artistic design (1986), then obtained the qualification of a teacher of art education and handicraft from the Tallinn Pedagogical Institute (1991), and has worked since 1991 at the Tartu Art School as a teacher of drawing and print (in the years 2001–2010 also as a lecturer in the print speciality). Kalli Kalde has participated in exhibitions since 1988. She is a member of the Estonian Print Artists' Association and the Tartu Artists' Association.
