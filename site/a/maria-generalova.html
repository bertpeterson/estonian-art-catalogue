Maria Generalova

1991–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Maria Generalova (b. 1991 in Tallinn) is a young painter who graduated in 2013 from the bachelor's programme in painting at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Her work is focused on figurative composition; her paintings reflect various socially topical global issues. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
