Kristina Viin

1985–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Kristina Viin (born 22 November 1985 in Võru) is an Estonian artist, illustrator and poet. She belongs to the Tartu-affiliated women artists' group Delegatsioon K. Viin graduated from the Tartu Art College as a sculptor and has studied theology at the University of Tartu (since 2015). She has published the poetry collections "Nõtkel elevandisammul" (2014) and "Isepüüdleja" (2018), as well as poems in Looming, Vikerkaar, Müürileht, Värske Rõhk and elsewhere in anthologies, as well as art reviews and articles. Kristina Viin has illustrated poetry collections, children's books and publications, and has exhibited in Estonia and Finland. She has designed the stage set for the Tartu Student Theatre's production "Daniil Harms ehk Geeniuse elu". Viin is a member of the Estonian Academic Oriental Society and the Estonian Writers' Union.
