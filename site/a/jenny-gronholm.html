Jenny Grönholm

1988–
Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vaal galerii

Jenny Grönholm (b. 1988) graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts, Faculty of Fine Arts, with a bachelor's degree in graphic art in 2015, and a master's degree (sculpture and installation) in 2018. Grönholm was born in Finland but lives and works in Tallinn. She has participated in solo and group exhibitions both in Estonia and in Finland.
