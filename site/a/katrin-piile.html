Katrin Piile

1987–
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Tütar galerii

Katrin Piile (1987) is a painter, performance artist and draughtsman who graduated in painting from the Estonian Academy of Arts in 2010, has been exhibiting since 2006, and works within the ranks of Metropol, one of the most distinctive phenomena of the Estonian art scene, an artists' commune. Katrin Piile's paintings and drawings are characterised by a painterly and emotional trickster-type hyperrealism, precision in detail, a large volume of work, and a certain dramatic quality that can be associated with the influences of comic culture.
