Günther Reindorff

1889–1974 · Estonian and Soviet artist and graphic designer (1889–1974)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Saint Petersburg Stieglitz State Academy of Art and Design

Born into the family of a customs official, in 1897 the family moved to Tallinn; in 1914-1915 he travelled in France, Spain and Scandinavia. In 1915-1919 Reindorff worked at a securities printing house in Petrograd and Moscow. In 1920 he opted for the Republic of Estonia. In 1920-1943 he was a lecturer at the State School of Applied Art, initially a teacher, from 1922 head of the printmaking workshop, and in 1936 head of the printmaking department. He founded the school's printmaking class, the lithography workshop (1920) and the printing and zincography workshop (1923). In 1922-1932 he worked as a consultant in cooperation with the State Printing House, and also worked with the advertising agencies Ilo, Ole and Gravis. In 1932 he was a founding member of the Applied Art Society RaKÜ. In 1950-1958 he was a lecturer at ERKI (EKA) (professor in 1951) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
