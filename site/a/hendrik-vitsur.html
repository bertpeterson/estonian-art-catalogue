Hendrik Vitsur

1911–1962 · Estonian artist (1911-1961)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

Book graphic artist, calligrapher and poster artist. Graduated in 1931 as an applied graphic artist from the State School of Applied Art. During World War II was in the Russian rear in Yaroslavl, was a member of the board of the ESSR Artists' Union 1945-1950, was one of the organisers and artistic director of the Art Products Combine, worked at the Estonian State Publishing House 1949-1954.
