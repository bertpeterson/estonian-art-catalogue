Aleksander Koemets

1912–1988 · Estonian printmaker (1912–1988)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Aleksander Koemets was born in Moscow into a factory worker's family; at the beginning of the First World War his father returned with his son to his native land, Estonia, where A. Koemets received his elementary education in Võru and Pärnu. After finishing evening secondary school in Tartu in 1931, he studied from 1936 at the State School of Applied Arts, graduating in 1941 from the J. Koort State School of Applied Art. He furthered his education in France and Belgium. He was mobilised into the Red Army, was a front-line combatant in the Second World War, and was wounded. He continued his art studies and graduated in 1948 from the Art Institute (EKA) as a book graphic artist. He was an art editor at the publishing house Ilukirjandus ja Kunst, and from 1957 to 1970 chief artist of the publishing house Kunst. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
