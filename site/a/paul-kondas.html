Paul Kondas

1900–1985 · Estonian painter (1900-1985)
Viljandi Muuseum

trained at University of Tartu

In the foreground of the painting a river with naked women bathing in it. In total there are 14 women in the picture, of which 6 are in the water. Most of the women (7) sit or lie on white towels on the right bank of the river. On the left bank a woman in underwear is getting dressed. At the bottom left one of the women has a child in her arms. The upper background of the painting is green, with a sandy-coloured edge of the riverbank. On the right side a tree, in the distance bushes of various sizes. The sky is also visible, uniformly blue.
