Aureelia Mitt


NOBA

Aureelia Mitt is a painter who has studied at the University of South Wales and completed a degree in sustainable design at Kingston School of Art in London. Her landscape paintings are compositions of memories – a mixture of the seen, dreams and imaginings. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
