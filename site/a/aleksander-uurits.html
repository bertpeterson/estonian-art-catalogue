Aleksander Uurits

1888–1918 · Estonian artist (1888-1918)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

He was born in Tallinn into the family of a cabman, studied art in 1903–1906 at Ants Laikmaa's studio school and in 1910–1916 in St. Petersburg at the school of the Society for the Advancement of the Arts under Nikolai Roerich and Ivan Bilibin. He furthered his education in 1910–1911 in Paris and in 1913 in St. Petersburg. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
