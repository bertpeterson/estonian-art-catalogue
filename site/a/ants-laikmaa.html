Ants Laikmaa

1866–1942 · Estonian painter (1866-1942)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid

trained at Kunstakademie Düsseldorf

From 1893 to 1898 she attended Cornelia Niclasen's four-, later five-grade German girls' private school. After finishing her studies she worked as a salesperson in a bookshop. She began writing poetry in German at the age of 13. In 1902 she married the Estonian bookkeeper Carl Eduard Friedrich Hacker, and the young couple moved to Kutino, a suburb of Moscow. They had 2 children. In 1904 Marie fell in love with the Estonian painter Ants Laikmaa. Laikmaa persuaded Marie to translate her poems into Estonian so that they could later be published in a local newspaper. In 1906 Under returned to Tallinn. In 1913 she met Artur Adson, who became her literary editor. Adson gathered together Marie's poetic material in order to publish her first collection of poetry. In 1924 Marie divorced Carl Hacker and married Adson. Marie Under belonged to the group "Siuru", which cultivated personal, emotional poetry, and achieved fame as a poet with her debut collection "Sonetid" ["Sonnets"], published in 1917. Under is one of the greatest Estonian lyric poets. Her evocative and emotion-filled work portrays love and nature with a thirst for life. Her later, more contemplative poetry ponders the light and shadow sides of life. In 1944 Under fled to Sweden. In the verses she wrote there one can sense a longing for home. Under's poetry has been translated into many languages. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
