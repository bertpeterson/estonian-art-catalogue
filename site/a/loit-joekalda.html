Loit Jõekalda

1951– · Estonian printmaker (born 1951)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

Loit Jõekalda (b. 1951) graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute in photography and print (1986). He is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and chairman of the Estonian Print Artists' Association, a member of the Estonian Ancient Art Society (1987), the Estonian Sami Association (1988), the Finnish Ancient Art Society (1990) and the artists' association YDI (1998). Jõekalda has participated in art exhibitions since 1979, has organised solo exhibitions at home and abroad, and has curated numerous exhibitions and events related to print and ancient art.
