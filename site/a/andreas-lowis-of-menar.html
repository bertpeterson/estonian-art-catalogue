Andreas Löwis of Menar

1778–1839
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Andreas von Löwis of Menar was born on 4 January 1778 (at the end of 1877 according to the old calendar) at Vanamõisa in Kirbla Parish, Läänemaa, the son of a retired general. In 1794–97 he was in military service in St. Petersburg, serving as a warrant officer in the cavalry. In 1801 he went to study at Jena and in 1803 at the University of Heidelberg, and in 1806–1808 studied at the Schwetzingen Forestry Institute. In 1811 von Löwis of Menar was elected secretary of the Livonian Public Benefit and Economic Society based in Riga. He organised its activities and was the editor of the society's publications. He subsequently moved to Tartu and probably studied art under C. A. Senff. In 1816–39 he took part in the trigonometric survey of Livonia and the compiling of maps carried out under the direction of Friedrich Georg Wilhelm Struve. His most important work is his own illustrated survey of the knightly castles of Livonia: Denkmäler aus der Vorzeit Liv- und Estlands I-II (1821, 1827). He died at Kaipen (Keipene) manor in Latvia/Livonia. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
