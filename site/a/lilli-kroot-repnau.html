Lilli-Krõõt Repnau

1982–
Artrovert; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Vaal galerii

Lilli-Krõõt Repnau (b. 1982) graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts, Department of Graphic Art, in 2005, and completed her master's studies in the field of animation in 2015. She has also furthered her studies in Switzerland, at the video department of the Lucerne University of Art and Design. In 2010 Lilli-Krõõt Repnau's graphic art was recognised with the Edmund Valtman Scholarship. She is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Association of Printmakers.
