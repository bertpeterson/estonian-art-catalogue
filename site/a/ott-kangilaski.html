trained at Pallas

He studied at Arumetsa Primary School and at the Tartu Boys' Science Gymnasium, graduating from gymnasium in Viljandi. From 1931 to 1935 he studied art and literary history and folklore at the Faculty of Philosophy of the University of Tartu. In 1934 he studied at the Pallas Higher Art School in Ado Vabbe's graphic art studio. In 1940-1941 he studied at the Konrad Mägi State Higher Art School. In 1943 he graduated from the Higher Courses of Fine Art in graphic art. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
