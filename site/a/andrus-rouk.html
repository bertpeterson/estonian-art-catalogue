Andrus Rõuk

1957– · Estonian poet, artist (born 1957)
Allee galerii; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

After graduating from what is now Pelgulinna Gymnasium in 1976, he started working at the then Youth Theatre as an advertising designer. With his geometric gouache drawings, Andrus Rõuk reached his first joint exhibition in 1977 at the old café of the University of Tartu. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
