Olesja Semenkova

1992–
NOBA

Olesja Semenkova (1992) lives and works in Tallinn, Estonia. Her works were exhibited in Tallinn, Estonia and in Brussels, Belgium. Olesja experiments with and includes different mediums into one artwork. Her practice can also be called installation art as she enjoys questioning and playing with the spectator's role in the exhibition space. Finished Tallinn University in 2015 as Bachelor of Arts in Humanities, from 2017 studying in Estonian Academy of Arts, Contemporary Arts, MA, improved her skills at LUCA school of Arts, Fine Art department, Brussels.
