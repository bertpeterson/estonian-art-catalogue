Anne Parmasto

1952– · Estonian painter (born 1952)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Anne Parmasto (b. 1952) graduated from the Estonian SSR State Art Institute in 1981 as a painter (with a supplementary specialty in theatre decoration). Since 1999 Parmasto has been an associate professor of painting at the Institute of Cultural Sciences and Arts of the University of Tartu. Since 1984 Parmasto has held 35 solo exhibitions in Estonia, Germany and the USA and has participated in more than 300 group and overview exhibitions in Estonia, Austria, Belgium, Luxembourg, Lithuania, Italy, Sweden, Germany (incl. ART COLOGNE), Finland, Turkey, the USA, Belarus, Russia. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
