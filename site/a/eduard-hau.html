Eduard Hau

1807–1888 · Baltic German painter and graphic artist (1807-1888)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

member of Imperial Academy of Arts · movement academic art · trained at Dresden Academy of Fine Arts

Having studied the painter's craft, G. F. Schlater arrived in Tartu from Riga in 1828, where in 1832 he founded a lithography workshop. In addition to his significant activity in the field of graphic art, he was also a painter and a drawing teacher at the Tartu gymnasium and the veterinary institute. In 1857 he sold his lithography press to Louis Höflinger and became a photographer. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
