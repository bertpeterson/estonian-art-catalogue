Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

1956 According to Paul Ambur's periodisation, the second period of ex-libris creation began; from 1957 he took part in all major and most thematic Soviet Estonian ex-libris exhibitions. As an ex-libris artist he also became known outside the Soviet Union; his ex-libris were acquired into private collections in the USSR, the Eastern Bloc, Western Europe, America and Australia (among others he made an ex-libris for Gianni Mantero, president of the International Federation of Ex-libris Societies) and were exhibited at international ex-libris exhibitions.
