Aulin Rimm

1930–1999 · Estonian sculptor (1930-1999)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Known primarily as a portraitist. His portraits are characterised by the vigorous faceted form typical of the 1960s, which by the end of the decade became more lyrical and naturalistic, this above all in sculptures portraying women. He has also created figural plastic works, mainly female nudes, as well as portrait reliefs of scholars connected with the University of Tartu in the university's buildings, and monuments to Estonian cultural figures.
