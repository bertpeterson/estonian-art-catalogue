Jüri Mildeberg

1965– · Estonian artist (born 1965)
Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vernissage

Jüri Mildeberg is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association, the Estonian Association of Graphic Designers, and the Estonian branch of IBBY. He has illustrated children's books, collaborated with magazines, and created the identity of Estonian Public Broadcasting in 2005. In addition, he painted the window shutters of the hall of the Estonian Children's Literature Centre (2008) and created ex libris. His works belong to the art collection of the National Library of Estonia and to the illustration collection of the Estonian Children's Literature Centre.
