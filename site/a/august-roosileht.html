August Roosileht

1887–1941 · Estonian graphic artist (1887-1941)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Viljandi Muuseum

Grew up in Järvamaa, in Nõmküla parish, at Remmiku farm. Studied art from 1901 at the drawing courses of the Paide Karskuse Kuratoorium, led by Rudolf Lepik, in 1906 at the Stieglitz School of Technical Drawing in St. Petersburg, in 1907 in the studios of Michael Zeno Diemer and Heinrich Heidner in Munich. In 1910 continued his studies in St. Petersburg at the art school of the Society for the Encouragement of the Arts, where his teacher was Ivan Bilibin. In 1913 received the qualification of drawing teacher. In 1909 exhibited for the first time at an art exhibition in Tartu. During the First World War he served in the Tsarist army, making front-line drawings. In 1918 Roosileht was the drawing teacher at Põltsamaa Realgymnasium, from 1919 a teacher at Paide Gymnasium. In 1920 married Magda Angelus. Perished by shooting in August 1941. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
