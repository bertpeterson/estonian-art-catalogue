Kaidi Kaasik

1982– · Estonian artist (born 1982)
Artrovert; NOBA

Kaidi Kaasik is a graphic designer who works with abstract painting. She has studied performance art at Non Grata, graphic art at EKA and documentary film at BFM. Her calligraphic abstract mixed-media paintings are colourful and contrasting, bringing organised chaos into the world. Her painting series NO MIND denotes the state of mind in which she creates. It is a creative flow free of ego and everyday life. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
