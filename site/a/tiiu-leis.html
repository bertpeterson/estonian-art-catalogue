Tiiu Leis

1967–
NOBA

Tiiu Leis (b. 1967) is an art teacher and artist, critic, who lives and works in Pärnu, Estonia and Pori, Finland. Graduated from Tallinn University with a degree in art education, JT (1994). 2016-2019 supervisor of animation workshops at Pärnu Museum, 2017-2019 participated in the Erasmus+ ProArt art project in Poland, Latvia, Slovakia, Italy and a web design course in Luxembourg. Organised the Erasmus+ art project 2026 in Treimani. 2017-2018 organiser/instructor of an animation camp in Pärnu and painting camps in Kihnu 2013-2020, Muhu 2023 and Treimani 2024-2026. Since 2020 she has had her own art studio, and also works with children and adults with special needs. 1995-1997 she was head of the library at the Pärnu Chaplin Art Centre and organised exhibitions, poetry, literature and film evenings. Leis was the chief organiser of the 1st Estonian video-format film festival Do-Re-Vi (documentary, advertising and video film) at the Mai Culture Centre in Pärnu. Tiiu Leis is Teacher of the Year 2016 in Pärnu and a nominee for Youth Worker of the Year 2016 nationally, and received the 2017 culture award for organising the exhibition "Rao Heidmets's Animaworld" at the Pärnu Museum. She has participated in the international performance exhibition "Man and Woman", in recycling exhibitions at the Museum of New Art in Pärnu and in overview exhibitions in Pärnu.
