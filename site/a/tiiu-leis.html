Tiiu Leis

1967–
NOBA

Tiiu Leis (b. 1967) is an art teacher and artist, critic, who lives and works in Pärnu, Estonia and Pori, Finland. She graduated from Tallinn University in art education JT (1994). 2016-2019 animation workshop instructor at Pärnu Museum, 2017-2019 participated in the Erasmus+ ProArt art project in Poland, Latvia, Slovakia, Italy and a web design course in Luxembourg. Organised the Erasmus+ art project 2026 in Treimani. 2017-2018 organiser/instructor of animation camp in Pärnu and painting camps in Kihnu 2013-2020, Muhu 2023 and Treimani 2024-2026. Since 2020 she has had her own art studio, and also works with children and adults with special needs. 1995-1997 she was the head of the library at the Pärnu Chaplin Art Centre and organised exhibitions, poetry, literature and film evenings. Leis was the chief organiser of the 1st Estonian film festival Do-Re-Vi (documentary, advertising and video film) in video format at the Mai Culture Centre in Pärnu. Tiiu Leis is the 2016 Teacher of Pärnu and was nominated for 2016 Youth Worker of the Republic, and received the 2017 culture award for organising the exhibition "Rao Heidmets's Animaworld" at the Pärnu Museum. She has participated in the international action exhibition "Man and Woman", reuse exhibitions at the Museum of New Art in Pärnu and overview exhibitions in Pärnu.
