Albert Nikolajevitš Benois

1851–1936
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum

As he also found recognition at court, he was appointed to positions of great responsibility. Thus he was the first curator of the Russian Museum in St. Petersburg (from 1895) and inspector of the art department of the Department of Trade and Manufactures (later the Ministry).
