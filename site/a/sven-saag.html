Sven Saag

1968– · Estonian artist (born 1968)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Narva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Sven Saag (b. 1968) is an Estonian painter and graphic artist. He studied painting at the Chair of Painting of the University of Tartu from 1991 to 1994. The artist has participated in numerous exhibitions both at home and abroad. The artist says of his painting: "Like a restorer who removes layers of paint and finds ever new figures, views, faces beneath them, one can also approach one's own paintings in depth — scrape the paintings and discover ever new and old images beneath the layers of paint." Since 2001 a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and since 2008 a member of the Estonian Painters' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
