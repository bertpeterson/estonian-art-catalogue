Krista Leesi

1966–
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Krista Leesi has studied textile art at the Estonian Academy of Arts. She actively participates in exhibitions and has long-standing teaching experience at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Leesi's body of work includes both unique works and practical small series. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
