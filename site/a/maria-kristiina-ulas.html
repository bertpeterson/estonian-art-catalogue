Maria-Kristiina Ulas

1965– · Estonian painter (born 1965)
Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Maria-Kristiina Ulas graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in 1991 in the field of graphic art, following in the footsteps of her artist parents – her mother Concordia Klar (1938–2004) and father Peeter Ulas (1934–2008) are well-known Estonian graphic artists.
