Erika Haggi

1922–2009 · Estonian sculptor (1922-2009)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

In 1955 he completed a full-figure statue of the then prima ballerina Helmi Puur. In the following decades, too, Erika Haggi dedicated herself mainly to portraying Estonian cultural figures. From her oeuvre one recalls the Nordic-granite works August Jansen, Heino Eller, Tiit Kuusik, and the granite bust of Georg Ots unveiled in the foyer of Estonia in his jubilee year 1977. She has also portrayed well-known athletes. Alongside portraiture, the sculptor was also interested in the decorative genre. Among her decorative works, one might first mention the dark granite torso "The Sailor's Bride", which adorns the green area in front of the Tallinn Art Hall.
