Theodor Albert Sprengel

1832–1900 · Baltic German painter (1832-1900)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii

Born in the Harz, the so-called Saxon Switzerland, into a pastor's family, studied 1848-52 at the Dresden and 1852-56 at the Düsseldorf Art Academy. Lived in Vyborg 1855-56, then in Tallinn. Was a drawing teacher at the Tallinn Cathedral School 1872-77. Also worked as a painter, draughtsman, art critic, poet. Buried at Mõigu Cemetery.
