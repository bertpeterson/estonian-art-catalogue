Peeter Laurits

1962– · Estonian artist (born 1962)
Artrovert; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum

Born 30.07.1947. Estonian actor and pedagogue. Father a dairyman, mother a dairy laboratory assistant. Graduated from Rakvere 1st Secondary School in 1965, studied at Tallinn Polytechnic Institute in 1965–68, and graduated from the acting department of the Tallinn State Conservatory in 1972. Worked as an actor at the Drama Theatre in 1972–75 and 1978–95, was a lecturer in theatre studies at the Tallinn Pedagogical Institute in 1975–80 and at the Estonian Institute of Humanities in 1988–2004; from 1995 a teacher at the Old Town Educational Collegium and from 1997 also at its Theatre School (stage speech etc.). Wrote the play "Olevused" (1980) together with Juhan Viiding, compiled and directed as a group work "Lavakavu" (I 1982, II 1984) and the programme "Kallis õhtu" (I 1992, II 2014). Sang (together with J. Viiding and Lembit Ulfsak) in the ensemble Amor Trio, performed at the Viru Variety, played in TV productions ("Mõmmi ja aabits", 1973–77 and 1998–99) and in film ("Aeg maha!", 1975, Eesti Telefilm), took part in the concert series "Tornikell minu külas" (1979).
