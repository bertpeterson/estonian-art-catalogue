Hermann Eduard Hartmann

1817–1881 · Baltic German painter (1817-1881)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

H. E. Hartmann studied art in Tartu under A. M. Hagen, at the Düsseldorf Academy of Arts and in Munich. In 1841–44 he worked in Latvia as a schoolteacher in Bērzaine, from 1845 in Tartu as a drawing teacher and city treasury accountant. In 1845 he received the title of free artist from the St. Petersburg Academy of Arts, and from 1860 he was conservator of the collections of the Learned Estonian Society. He painted portraits and landscapes, but especially significant is his Estica-themed graphic art. Hartmann's woodcuts of Estonians appeared in the publications: Talurahva Postimees, Tartu Postimees, Das Inland (in German), Pasaule (in Latvian), etc. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
