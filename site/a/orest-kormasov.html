Orest Kormašov

1964–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vaal galerii

Orest Kormašov (1964) is a painter, icon scholar and restorer. His paintings are inspired by nature, people and moments of everyday life, but reflect deeper layers of life and offer food for thought. Kormašov graduated from ERKI in the field of painting (1990), obtained a master's degree in art history at Tallinn University (2001) and worked for a long time at the Institute of Art there. He has participated in exhibitions and also organised them, published articles, worked on the conservation of ancient Russian icon art, and since 2014 has been conducting a number of thematic tours at the Icon Museum. Kormašov is a member of the Estonian Painters' Association and the Estonian Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
