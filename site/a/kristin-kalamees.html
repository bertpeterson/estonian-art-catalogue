Kristin Kalamees


Eesti Kunstimuuseum

The artist is a saviour, a comforter. When no one needs saving, the saviour must somehow manage — staging situations for himself in which he is needed and gathering around him young disciples who might carry forward his ideas. Those who need saving search among false rescue possibilities. Both are in an awkward situation, where one must advertise himself to the other through a performance. The slides do not actually offer ready-made solutions, but hint at the beginning of the rescue process, its possibility. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
