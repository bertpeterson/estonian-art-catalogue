Lilian Mosolainen

1961– · Estonian artist (born 1961)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

In 1980 he graduated from the Tartu Art School. In 1991 he graduated from the Tallinn Art University as a painter. Lecturer at the Tallinn Art School. In 1987 he took part in a youth exhibition, in 1989 in a national exhibition. In 1992 became a member of the Artists' Union. Solo exhibitions: 1989 at G-Gallery, 1991 at Galerie Viro, Helsinki; 1992 Tokko&Arrak; 1993 in Espoo; 1994 KH Gallery; 1997 Sammas Gallery; 1998 "Estonia" theatre; 2002 Narva Hermann Castle; 2004 Vaal Gallery.
