Taje Paldrok


NOBA

Taje Paldrok is a performance, installation and new media artist. She acquired her art education at Academia Non Grata, at the Kuvataideakatemia in Helsinki, at the Estonian Academy of Arts and at the Universität der Künste Berlin. She has been one of the main driving forces of the international Non Grata Performance Group since 1998. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
