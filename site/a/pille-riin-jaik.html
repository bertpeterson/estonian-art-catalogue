Pille-Riin Jaik

1991–
NOBA

Pille-Riin Jaik (born 1991 in Tallinn) is an interdisciplinary artist living in Vienna, who works mostly in the fields of video art and performance, but also frequently creates sculptures and installations. She has earned a Bachelor's degree in Photography at the Estonian Academy of Arts, a Master's degree in Art and Media (professor Constanze Ruhm) at the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna, and is currently working there as a PhD in Practice candidate, researching storytelling and political landscape narratives in the Baltics. She is also a member of the video art collective Golden Pixel. In her works she is mostly interested in hypertext, strange places, weeds, textiles and fabric patterns, leftover and unused materials or ideas in a queer-feminist and class-conscious context.
