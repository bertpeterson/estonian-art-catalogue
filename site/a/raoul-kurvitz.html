Raoul Kurvitz

1961– · Estonian artist (born 1961)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Vaal galerii; Veneetsia biennaal, Eesti paviljon

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Raoul Kurvitz studied architecture at ERKI (present-day Estonian Academy of Arts), graduating in 1984. Together with Urmas Muru they formed the group Rühm T in 1986, soon joined by architect Peeter Pere as well as several other creative people.
