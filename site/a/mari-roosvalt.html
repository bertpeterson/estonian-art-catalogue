Mari Roosvalt

1945– · Estonian artist (born 1945)
Allee galerii; Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Born in Tallinn in 1945. He received his art education at the Estonian State Art Institute, graduating in painting in 1969. Member of the Estonian Artists' Union since 1979. Has taken part in exhibitions since 1970. Since 1992 a lecturer at the Estonian Academy of Arts. Member of the Board of the Estonian Painters' Association since 1997. Member of the Estonian Watercolourists' Association 2002. In 2006 chairman of the Estonian Painters' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
