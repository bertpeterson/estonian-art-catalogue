Martin Buschmann


NOBA

Martin Buschmann completed his master's studies in Contemporary Art at the Estonian Academy of Arts in 2021, and his bachelor's studies in Photography in 2019. Buschmann uses photography and installation as means of expression, exploring from a critical perspective various gender representations, game theories and simulacra.
