Katrin Valgemäe

1979–
NOBA; Ruki galerii

Katrin Valgemäe (b. 1979), who spices classical painting technique with pop art, kitsch and camp, is a freelance artist who lives and works in Tallinn. Valgemäe has studied painting and ceramics at the Estonian Academy of Arts, and furthered her studies at the Art Academy of Latvia. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
