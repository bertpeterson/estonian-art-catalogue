Aarne Mesikäpp

1939– · Estonian illustrator and printmaker (born 1939)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Aarne Mesikäpp graduated from Tartu Art School in the field of theatre decoration and from ERKI in the field of graphic art. Since 1967 he has worked at the Estonian Academy of Arts as a lecturer and master craftsman. He has participated in numerous joint exhibitions and has also organised solo exhibitions.
