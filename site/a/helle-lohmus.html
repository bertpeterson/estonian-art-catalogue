Helle Lõhmus

1962–
Haus Galerii; NOBA

Helle Lõhmus (b. 1962) is a painter living and working in Tartu, who also engages in drawing, graphic art and sculpture. She has completed a painting degree at the University of Tartu (BA, 2015) and an art and technology teacher's degree (MA, 2022). She is a member of the Estonian Artists' Association, the Estonian Painters' Association and NAS. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
