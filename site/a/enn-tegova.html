Enn Tegova

1946– · Estonian painter (born 1946)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Tartu Art School

Enn Tegova's (b. 1946 in Tartu) creative activity has been consistently connected with Tartu, to whose art life he has made a strong and lasting contribution. His paintings are characterised by a strong metaphysical approach, a search for magical and mythical origins, influenced, among other things, by the primeval nature of his home region.
