Henn Roode

1924–1974 · Estonian painter (1924-1974)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

In 1943 he graduated from the Jakob Westholm Gymnasium in Tallinn. In 1944–1947 he studied at the Tartu State Art Institute (lecturers Ado Vabbe and Elmar Kits). In the autumn semester of 1947 he studied at the Tallinn State Institute of Applied Art (lecturers Johannes Võerahansu and Lepo Mikko). In 1956–1959 he studied at the Estonian State Art Institute, graduating with a diploma in painting. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
