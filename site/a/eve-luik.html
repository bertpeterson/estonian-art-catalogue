Eve Luik

1976–
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Linnamuuseum

Eve Luik (1949) has worked in painting and printmaking since 1965, working at the time as an advertising artist. Since 1992 Eve Luik has been a member of the Estonian Artists' Association. Since the same year she has been a teacher at the Tartu Children's Art School. Eve Luik's works have been exhibited at exhibitions in Estonia, Finland, Italy, France, Switzerland, China, Japan, as well as reproduced on postcards, posters, in magazines and as illustrations in books. Her work, with its magical density and meditative depth, has found its way into many private collections. Eve has also fulfilled herself as an interior designer, as well as a fashion designer and make-up artist in Tartu beauty salons. For a long time the artist has also practised Chi-Jog-Kata, which imitates Eastern martial arts, and has passed on her skills as a trainer at the Tartu University Sports Hall.
