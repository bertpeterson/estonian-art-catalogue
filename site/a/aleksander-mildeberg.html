Aleksander Mildeberg

1902–1977 · Estonian printmaker and photographer (1902–1977)
Allee galerii; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Mildeberg worked as a photographer and applied artist from 1925. In 1941 he was evacuated to the rear of the Soviet Union, to Bashkiria, where he worked as a farm labourer in the Birsk district. In 1942-1945 he was a front-line artist and photographer in the Estonian Rifle Corps of the Red Army. In 1945 he became a member of the Artists' Union. After returning to Tallinn, Mildeberg worked in 1945 as a photo correspondent for Rahva Hääle, in 1945-1946 Mildeberg was the technical editor of the magazine Pikker, later a freelance artist. He preferred graphic techniques, made caricatures and cartoons, and illustrated books. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
