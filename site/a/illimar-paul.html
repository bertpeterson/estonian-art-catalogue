Illimar Paul

1945– · Estonian painter (born 1945)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Pärnu Muuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Illimar Paul (b. 1945) began his graphic art studies at the Estonian Academy of Arts (ERKI) in 1964. He continued his studies at the Leipzig Higher School of Graphic Arts and Book Design, from which he graduated with a diploma in 1971. He has taken part in more than 300 joint exhibitions both at home and abroad, and has had 68 solo exhibitions. "Illimar Paul's townscapes made in the hyperrealistic style of the 1970s in the serigraphy technique belong not only to the classics of our graphic art, but were at the time among the most innovative works of graphic art, both in terms of technique and manner of depiction. In his work Paul has used several techniques – besides silkscreen printing, other graphic techniques (linocut), but also watercolour, coloured pencil drawing, mixing them together, moving from a two-dimensional mode of presentation into space, making three-dimensional collages, etc., but his love of detail has survived to this day. Moreover, detail has always meant something very personal and emotional to him." Reet Varblane, 2015. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
