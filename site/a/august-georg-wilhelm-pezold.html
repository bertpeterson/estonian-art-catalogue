August Georg Wilhelm Pezold

1794–1859
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Narva Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

He was born as the son of a district doctor in Rakvere, and was the stepson of Count Rehbinder, the owner of Udriku manor. He studied at the Tallinn Cathedral School and, from 1812 to 1814, medicine at the University of Tartu. In 1814 he went on a study trip to Berlin, then in 1815-1816 to Vienna, and lived in 1817-1820 in Italy, Germany, Switzerland, England and France. In 1821-1823 he lived in various places in Estonia and Latvia, and in 1823-1830 he was an art teacher at the Smolny Institute in St. Petersburg. In 1827 he travelled in Finland. In 1839 he became a candidate for academician at the St. Petersburg Academy of Arts. From 1842 he lived permanently in St. Petersburg. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
