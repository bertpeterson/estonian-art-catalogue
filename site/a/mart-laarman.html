Märt Laarman

1896–1979 · Estonian printmaker and painter (1896-1979)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum

trained at Atelier School of Ants Laikmaa

In 1924 he joined the Estonian Artists' Group and became its spiritual leader, in 1928 he edited the EKR's programmatic almanac Uue Kunsti Raamat (The Book of New Art), in 1926-1927 the Estonian Art yearbook, and in 1928-1929 the youth magazine Taie. He participated in the exhibitions of EKKKY and RaKÜ. He published art criticism in the newspapers and magazines Olion, Looming, and Kunst ja Kirjandus. He worked as a teacher in Tallinn schools until 1945 (J. Westholm Gymnasium, among others), after which he was a freelance artist. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
