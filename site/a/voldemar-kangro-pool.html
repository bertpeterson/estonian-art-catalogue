Voldemar Kangro-Pool

1889–1943 · Estonian printmaker and painter (1889-1943)
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii; Vernissage

trained at Anton Ažbe art school · trained at Pallas

Born at Vahi manor in Tartumaa, studied 1907–1909 in Kristjan Raud's studio in Tartu, in 1909 at Anton Ažbé's art school in Munich, 1910–1913 at the École des Beaux-Arts and free academies in Paris, in 1919 at Pallas and 1922–1923 at the Hamburg School of Applied Arts. Was a founding member of the art society Pallas. Was mobilized into the Red Army; his date of death in 1943 is unknown. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
