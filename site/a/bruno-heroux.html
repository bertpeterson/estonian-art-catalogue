Bruno Héroux

1868–1944 · German painter (1868-1944)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

He was at first a fashion-magazine illustrator and author of humorous illustrations. He also created anatomical drawings for an atlas of human and horse anatomy. He later cultivated lithography, etching and woodcut, as well as various techniques of watercolour and drypoint. He has created much applied graphic art, especially ex libris, but also portraits, nudes, interiors, etc. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
