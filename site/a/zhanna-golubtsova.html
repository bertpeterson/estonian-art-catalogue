Zhanna Golubtsova


NOBA

I studied for 6 years at the Ivangorod Art School and for 3 years in Russia at the Shadr Art School in Yekaterinburg. I work at the art school in Narva. In my free time I engage in creative work. I participate in group exhibitions of the Westerwall artists' community in various cities. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
