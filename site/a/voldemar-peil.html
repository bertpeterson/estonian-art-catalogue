Voldemar Peil

1907–1999 · Estonian scenographer (1907-1999)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Narva Muuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Pallas

Voldemar PEIL (1907, Narva – 1999) was an Estonian theatre artist who, among other things, also practised realistic easel painting (flower and landscape pictures as well as portraits). As a stage designer he designed about three hundred productions. His style is mainly of a genre character. Alongside painted scenery, Peil also made effective use of projection and lighting. He received his art education in the painting speciality at the Pallas Art School under Jaan Vahtra, Voldemar Mellik, Villem Ormisson and Ado Vabbe, graduating from the art school with the qualification of drawing teacher. In scenography he furthered his education at theatres in Moscow and Leningrad. /text by Liisi Aibel, 2013/. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
