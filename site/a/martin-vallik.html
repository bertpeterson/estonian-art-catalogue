Martin Vällik

1970–
NOBA

Martin Vällik's (1970) first solo exhibition took place at the University of Tartu Library in 1989, but his interest in astronomy, which began in childhood, started reaching his photographs from the 2010s onward. Martin Vällik works daily as a physics teacher at the Tallinn English College. He is a member of the Estonian Physical Society, the Estonian Astronomical Society and the Estonian Artists' Association. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
