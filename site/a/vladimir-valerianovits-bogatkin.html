Vladimir Valerianovitš Bogatkin

1922–1971
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

In 1934 he began his art studies in the studio of the graphic artist Aleksei Kravtšenko. From 1936 he studied at the Higher Art School of the All-Russian Academy of Arts in Leningrad (now the I. E. Repin Saint Petersburg Academic Institute of Painting, Sculpture and Architecture). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
