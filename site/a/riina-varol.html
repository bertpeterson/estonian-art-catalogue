Riina Varol

1979–
NOBA

Riina Varol (b. 1979) is an Estonian artist and photographer, known in the field of fashion and portrait photography, who does not, however, set herself limits in terms of media in her artistic expression, moving in the fields of photo manipulation, graphic art, painting, sculpture, space- and perception-based installations and performance. In her work Varol focuses on questions of sensory perception, functions of the subconscious, as well as animism and Eastern philosophy. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
