Maria von der Pahlen


Eesti Kunstimuuseum

Art lexicons do not mention her name, though there is information there about Olga. Unfortunately it is not known where Maria studied art. It is possible that they shared a home art teacher with their sister, and later she may have received guidance from her sister Olga. Maria v.d. Pahlen's connection to Palmse Manor is rather indirect. She did indeed descend from the Palmse branch of the Pahlen family, but not directly from Palmse Manor. Her father Diedrich was the owner of Voore (Forell) Manor, whose father, the artist's grandfather, Peter v.d. Pahlen (1777–1828), was likewise the owner of Voore Manor. Only Peter v.d. Pahlen's father, Arend Dietrich v.d. Pahlen (1738–1778), was born in Palmse as a child of the owner of Palmse Manor. However, being a younger son he did not inherit his birth manor, and instead became the owner of Koila (Koil) Manor in Virumaa". (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
