Carl Alexander Winkler

1860–1911
Allee galerii; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

Where his art teacher was Alexander Schlater. In 1870 and 1875 he was undergoing treatment in Germany and Austria. He studied at the Faculty of Linguistics of the University of Tartu 1879–1884 (cand phil), and in the same year stayed in a sanatorium in Switzerland, also in France. In 1889 he began studies in painting and watercolour. Teachers: Hermann Eschke in Berlin, Charles Way in Lausanne. In 1891–1893 Winkler was a home tutor at Kehtna Manor. In 1893 he took the teacher's examination at the St. Petersburg Academy of Arts. In 1898 he travelled in Norway, 1901–1904 in Dalmatia (Croatia) and Italy, in 1903 he stayed in Rome. In 1904 a solo exhibition in Tallinn at the Kanuti Guild Hall. In 1905 he stayed in Dresden. From 1911 he lived in Tallinn, where he led watercolour courses. He died in Dresden on 23.08.1911, and was buried near Tallinn at Mõigu cemetery (the grave has not survived) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
