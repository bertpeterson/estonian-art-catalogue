Kärt Hammer

1988–
Tütar galerii

Kärt Hammer (1988) has studied philosophy (BA 2011) and cultural theory (MA 2014) at Tallinn University and in her work has dealt with the paradox of the possibility and impossibility of interpreting a work of art. Hammer is an interdisciplinary artist who in her creative work mainly focuses on purely aesthetic and intuitive abstraction, avoiding excessive textuality.
