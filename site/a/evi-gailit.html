Evi Gailit

1952–
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Evi Gailit (1952) is a freelance artist. In 1999-2005 Gailit studied in Tartu in the studio of Konrad Mägi. She has participated in exhibitions since 2003. Gailit is a member of the Estonian Painters' Union, and also a member of the Tartu Artists' Union and KÜ Pallas. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
