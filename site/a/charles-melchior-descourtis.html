Charles-Melchior Descourtis

1753–1820 · French engraver (1753–1820)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Pärnu Muuseum

In the middle a woman in white clothing, barefoot, with a brownish shawl on her shoulders. She has stretched out her arms to the right, towards a girl, who strokes her cheek. A young man holds the girl around the waist, and with his other hand points to the right, towards a sailboat standing further away. From the left an older man and a woman come running.
