Johanna Mudist

1991– · Estonian painter (born 1991)
Artrovert; Vernissage

Johanna Mudist is an Estonian painter who currently lives and works in Tallinn. She obtained her bachelor's degree at the Tartu Art College (2010–2014) and her master's degree at the Estonian Academy of Arts (2014–2017). She has also studied under the Erasmus programme in Finland (2013) and Poland (2015–2016). She has participated in numerous group exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad (Finland, Poland, Lithuania, the Czech Republic). She has been a member of the Estonian Painters' Union since 2016.
