Roman Haavamägi

1891–1964 · Estonian sculptor, painter and graphic artist (1891-1964)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum; Haapsalu ja Läänemaa Muuseumid

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

In 1912 he entered the drawing courses of the Estonian Art Society in Tallinn, which in 1914 were renamed the Tallinn School of Applied Art, and also studied at Nikolai Triik's studio school until 1917, when he was mobilised into the army. In 1916 he became a member of the Estonian Art Society, and in 1917 a member of the art group Vikerla. In 1918–1921 he worked as a drawing teacher at the Paldiski Realgymnasium. In 1920 Haavamägi settled in Haapsalu, where he worked as a teacher of drawing and art history at the Läänemaa joint gymnasium, and thereafter as a freelance artist.
