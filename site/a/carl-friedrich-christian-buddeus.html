Carl Friedrich Christian Buddeus

1775–1864 · German painter (1775–1864)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum

C. F. C. Buddeus was born in Gotha, Thuringia, presumably into a family of French origin. He studied at a philanthropinum and worked as a private tutor. After 1809 he was a private tutor in Saaremaa, in the Pskov and St Petersburg governorates, and by 1825 at the latest he settled in Tallinn, where he married the daughter of the wine merchant Hartmuth. He lived on the Kopli peninsula. While in Estonia he engaged much in drawing. He died in Gotha. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
