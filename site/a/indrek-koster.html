Indrek Köster


NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Indrek Köster (b. 1970). Studied sculpture at the Tartu Art School and the Estonian Academy of Arts. Graduated in 2018 from EKA's Faculty of Fine Arts Master's programme. First exhibition participation at the Tartu Autumn Exhibition in 1992. More active exhibition activity in 2006–2021 with the artist duo Johnson and Johnson (together with Taavi Talve). Several solo exhibitions and dozens of group exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad. Member of the Estonian Artists' Association since 2011. Annual award of the Cultural Endowment's Visual and Applied Art Fund in 2013. Works in collections: Art Museum of Estonia, EKKM, Tartu Art Museum, Ludwig Museum.
