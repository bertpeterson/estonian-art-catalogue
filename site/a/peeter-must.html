Peeter Must


Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid

Peeter Must (b. 1967) graduated from ERKI in ceramics, but found his artistic outlet as a painter. In his work Peeter dedicates himself to the human being as a bearer and conveyor of values, directing the viewer to confront their own subconscious. As a member of the Estonian Artists' Association and the Estonian Painters' Union, he has participated in numerous exhibitions. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
