Pille Tael


NOBA

Pille Tael was born in Estonia in 1979. In 2001 she graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts in interior architecture and furniture design. In 2000 she won first place in the Riigikogu furniture design competition. In 2002, after graduating, she founded the company SAS Loft OÜ. In 2003-2005 she worked as curator of the Tallinn Botanical Garden. In 2005 she was a guest lecturer for the preparatory course in interior architecture and furniture design at the Estonian Academy of Arts. In 2006 she began building the SAS Loft studio, combining painting, graphic design, photography, animation and restoration. In 2008 she enrolled in the Master's programme in animation at the Estonian Academy of Arts. In the spring of 2009 she founded SA Jaama. The aim of SA Jaama is to restore the building of the Risti Railway Station, which was built in 1900. In 2009 she organised a memorial exhibition for Richard Sööt.
