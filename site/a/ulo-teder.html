Ülo Teder

Ülo TEDER (1923, Võru – 2008) was an Estonian painter who worked in watercolour, pastel, oil and tempera painting. He created landscapes and townscapes, figural compositions and still lifes in a vigorous style. Among other things he also designed film posters. Teder received his art education at the Tartu State Art Institute and at ERKI. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
