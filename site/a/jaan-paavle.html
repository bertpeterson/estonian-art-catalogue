Jaan Paavle

1940–2010 · Estonian writer and artist (1940-2010)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Jaan Paavle was an Estonian writer and artist. Paavle published six poetry collections, the first of which, "Vabaarmastus", appeared in 1972. At the age of 35 he entered the Tallinn Art Institute to study ceramics. In the 1990s Paavle became interested in performance art. He taught performance to children at the Tallinn Art School and worked as a guard at the Estonian Museum of Applied Art and Design. Jaan Paavle created ceramics, painted, wrote poems, plays, film and theatre criticism, made a few films himself and appeared in mass scenes of several films (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
