Jaan Koort

1883–1935 · Estonian sculptor, painter and ceramicist (1883-1935)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Saint Petersburg Stieglitz State Academy of Art and Design

A prominent Estonian sculptor, monumentalist, painter and ceramicist. Jaan Koort studied at Hugo Treffner's Gymnasium in Tartu and at the drawing courses of the German craftsmen's society, and in 1902-05 at Stieglitz's Art School in Peterburi. He lived in Paris in 1905-15, studying sculpture and painting. He worked in Moscow in 1915-16 and 1920. In 1919 he headed the South Estonian Art Protection Committee. In 1921-22 he was the head of the sculpture workshop of the Tallinn School of Applied Art. In 1927 he stayed in Australia. In 1934-35 he worked in Russia as an artist at the Gzhel ceramics factory.
