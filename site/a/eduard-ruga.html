Eduard Rüga

1903–1997 · Estonian printmaker and painter (1903-1997)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

trained at Pallas

Studied at Võru Gymnasium, 1928-30 and 1934-1939 in Tartu, at the art school Pallas, which he graduated from as a graphic artist. In 1942 he won the I prize in the Education Directorate's design competition for the design of the print series "Lõuna-Eesti inimene ja loodus" [South Estonian man and nature]. On 21.09.1944 he went into exile, lived in various DP camps in Germany, in 1949 moved to the USA. He directed the Estonian Cultural Fund in New York from 1958-1973.
