Eduard Ahas

1901–1944 · Estonian painter and art historian (1901-1944)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

trained at Pallas

Art education - 1918 R. Lepik's private studio. In 1919 entered the art school "Pallas". In 1926 obtained the qualification of drawing teacher. In 1931 entered the art school "Pallas" again. In 1931/1932 in N. Triik's studio, graduated in painting in 1932. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
