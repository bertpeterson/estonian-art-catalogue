Maarja Nõmmik


NOBA

Maarja Nõmmik (b. 1991) has obtained a bachelor's and master's degree in painting at the University of Tartu. In addition, she furthered her studies in painting, multimedia and conceptual textile at the Strzemiński Academy of Fine Arts in Łódź, Poland.
