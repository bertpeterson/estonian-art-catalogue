Eduard Ole

1898–1995 · Estonian painter (1898-1995)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

movement Expressionism · trained at Higher Art School at the Imperial Academy of Arts

Eduard Ole was born in 1898 in Valga County, Karula Parish, Kaagjärve Municipality, at the Saibasoo farm. He studied at pastor Wühner's four-grade church school and at Valga town school. He received his art education in 1914–1918 in Russia at the Penza private art school (a branch of the Saint Petersburg Academy of Arts), from which he graduated with the qualification of drawing teacher. After returning to Estonia, he worked from 1919 as a teacher at Rakvere Secondary School and the Teachers' Seminary, from 1922 at the Tartu Girls' Gymnasium and the Commercial and Trade School, and from 1924–1936 at Hugo Treffner Gymnasium; from 1936 at the Tallinn French Lyceum. In 1923, E. Ole was a founding member of the avant-garde Estonian Artists' Group; from 1927 he belonged to the Estonian Central Society of Visual Artists, later serving as the society's vice-chairman. E. Ole visited Paris repeatedly, for the first time in 1927. In 1937 he travelled together with Juhan Raudsepp to England, Belgium, France, Italy and Central Europe. In 1943 the artist fled Estonia for Finland; from 1944 he lived in Sweden, in Vällingby, West Stockholm, and worked at the Nordic Museum. In 1946, Ole, together with Eduard Wiiralt, undertook a trip to Lapland. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
