Valeri Vinogradov

1952–
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA

Valeri Vinogradov (b. 1952) is a painter. He studied at the Moscow children's art school and at the art school named after 1905. He continued his education in 1980 at the Estonian State Art Institute. He has mainly cultivated abstract painting with strong generalising force.
