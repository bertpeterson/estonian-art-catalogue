Lydia Laas

1909–1978 · Estonian sculptor (1909-1978)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Pallas

Her earlier free creative work focuses mainly on portraits of children and adolescents and on nude figures. Compositions made in the late 1930s and during the war years have a very delicate and poetic effect due to their fragile, expressive treatment of the figure. Lydia Laas's figurative works of the Soviet period acquired the simple, ascetic culture of form characteristic of the 1960s. From 1945 she worked as a freelance artist in Tallinn, being the only one of Estonia's first women sculptors who adapted to Soviet art life in the postwar decades.
