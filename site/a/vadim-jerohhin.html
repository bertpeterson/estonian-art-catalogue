Vadim Jerohhin

1962–
NOBA; Narva Muuseum

Vadim Jerohhin was born in 1962 in Kunda. He studied art from 2000 to 2015 in the "Stupeni" studio under teacher Vladislav Kuznetsov. Since 2009 a member of the Association of Russian Artists in Estonia. Since 2010 a member of the Narva artists' association "Vestervalli", since 2017 a member of its board. He is also a board member of two artists' associations, "Kunsti Sadam" and "Symbol 4". Kunsti Sadam has organised 8 international art camps, 16 solo exhibitions and nearly 200 group exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad (Russia, China, Italy, France, Serbia, Slovakia, USA) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
