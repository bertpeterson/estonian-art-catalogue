Otto Friedrich Pistohlkors noorem

1789–1843
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Rahva Muuseum

Otto Friedrich von Pistholkors IV (1789–1843) was the eldest son of the owner of the Rutikvere manor of the same name (1754–1831). He married Helene von Oettingen. He lost a leg to amputation during military service, and thereafter signed his pictures with a crutch mark. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
