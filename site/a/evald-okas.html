Evald Okas

1915–2011 · Estonian painter and printmaker (1915-2011)
Allee galerii; Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Was born into a joiner's family, obtained general education at Nõmme City 1st Primary School and Tallinn City Boys' Secondary School. He studied art from 1931-1937 at the State School of Applied Art (decorative painting), and from 1938-1941 at the State Higher Art School (painting). In 1941 he was mobilised into a labour battalion of the Red Army, and in 1944 belonged to the Yaroslavl collective of Estonian artists. After World War II he studied at the Moscow Academy of Arts. From 1944 he was a teacher at ERKI (TKÜ, EKA), and in 1993 became professor emeritus. In creating his works E. Okas travelled in 1958 to the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg, in 1961 to Italy, in 1962 to France, in 1963 to Japan. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
