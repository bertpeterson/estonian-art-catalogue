Aleksander Peek

1915–1982 · Estonian artist (1915-1982)
Allee galerii; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Meremuuseum; Haus Galerii; Pärnu Muuseum

Aleksander PEEK (1915, Kunda – 1982, Tallinn) was an Estonian theatre and painting artist and graphic artist, who obtained his education in stage design in Siberia at the Omsk Art School. He has been an artist at Estonia, later a freelance artist. /text by Liisi Aibel, 2013/. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
