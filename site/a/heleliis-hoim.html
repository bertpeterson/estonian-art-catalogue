Heleliis Hõim


Artrovert; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA

Heleliis Hõim is an interdisciplinary artist who works in parallel in different media. She has done several collaborative projects and solo exhibitions in Estonia, and has furthered her studies, alongside her MA studies in contemporary art at the Estonian Academy of Arts, at the Art Academy of Latvia and at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Milano in Italy. She has also participated in international group exhibitions and art residencies such as "NordArt'23", "Biennale Larnaca", "La Wayaka" in Panama and "Policlinica '20" in Ukraine.
