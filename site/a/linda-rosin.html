Linda Rosin

1904–1981 · Estonian sculptor (1904-1981)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Linda ROSIN (1904, Lesnoi, St. Petersburg – 1981, Tallinn) was an Estonian sculptor who, alongside sculpture, also practised oil painting. In sculpture she worked mainly in portraiture and figural composition, her portraits having a certain mannerist quality. Rosin received her art education at the State School of Applied Art in the women's handicraft speciality (also obtaining a drawing teacher's qualification), in applied sculpture at the Tallinn School of Applied Art, and in sculpture at the Tallinn State Institute of Applied Art, where after graduating she began working as a teacher.
