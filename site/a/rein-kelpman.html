Rein Kelpman

1960– · Estonian painter (born 1960)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Ruki galerii

Rein Kelpman (b. 1960 in Tartu) is a freelance artist, self-taught as a painter. Kelpman has participated in exhibitions since 1981, a member of the Estonian Artists' Association (Eesti Kunstnike Liit) since 1986. Has worked as a visiting lecturer at the University of Tartu.
