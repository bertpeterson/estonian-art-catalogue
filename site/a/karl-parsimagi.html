Karl Pärsimägi

1902–1942 · Estonian painter (1902-1942)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Académie Colarossi · trained at Pallas

In 1919, at the age of 17, he enrolled in the newly founded art school of the Art Society Pallas. He studied initially under Konrad Mägi, then under Nikolai Triik, and later under Ado Vabbe. In the spring-summer of 1923, he made a study trip to Germany under the guidance of Anton Starkopf. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
