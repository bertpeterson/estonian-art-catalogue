Reinhold Moeller

1847–1918
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Reinhold von Moeller studied zoology at the University of Tartu and Munich. Before 1875 he also studied at the Munich Academy of Art, after which he was a pupil of Hermann Baisch. He traveled in Western Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, hence many exotic views by him can be found (Mai Levin) (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
