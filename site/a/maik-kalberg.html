Maik Kalberg

1987–
NOBA

Maik Kalberg (born 1987 in Tartu) is an Estonian painter and filmmaker. He studied at Tartu Art Grammar School in a class with an art focus and obtained higher education in film art at the Baltic Film and Media School. He currently lives and works in Tallinn. As an artist, Maik wishes, both with his existence and his creative work, to create positive energy in the world and to seek, at the very least, even a useful and practical pattern of behaviour. A person's task in this world is not merely to burden and consume the environment in which they exist, but to give birth, through their presence here, to something uplifting.
