Olesja Katšanovskaja-Münd


Haus Galerii; NOBA

Olesya Münd's education and creative work are continuously connected with interdisciplinary pursuits. She has educational and work experience in the fields of fine arts, museum pedagogy and conservation. This has given the artist the opportunity to know the presence of art. Münd holds a bachelor's degree in fine arts from the University of Tartu and a master's degree in art pedagogy from the Estonian Academy of Arts. She is currently studying conservation and restoration in St. Petersburg at the Repin State Academy of Arts. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
