Enno Ootsing (b. 1940) has taken part in Estonian art life since 1964 as a printmaker, book artist and watercolourist, belonging to the group ANK '64, which held an important place in the innovative movement of Estonian art. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
