Denisa Štefanigová

1995–
NOBA

Denisa Štefanigová (b. 1995) is fresh graduates of the Estonian Academy of Arts in the Contemporary Art Department. With her last project, the Master thesis titled “Violet, show me how tender you can be? (Shades of Love & Desire & Infidelity & Truth)” she reflected on the topic of the sexual life of colors, of brushstrokes, through paintings, videos, puppets – a topic she keeps researching and working on. She previously graduated with a Bachelor degree from the University of Technology, Faculty of Fine Arts (FaVU) in Brno Czechia in 2019. Denisa has also studied in Facultad de Bellas Artes in Bilbao in 2017, and has been on artistic residency in Stuttgart in GEDOK Gallery on 2018. She is currently attending the Artist Residency Programme at Easttopics in Budapest, Hungary. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
