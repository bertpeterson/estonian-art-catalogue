Joseph Dominikus Oechs

1775–1836
Eesti Kunstimuuseum

He received his initial art education under his brother, the porcelain painter Anton Oechs, in Regensburg. From 1795 he studied in Nuremberg under the porcelain painter K. Trost, and then in Erfurt under the porcelain painter Bollermann, and in Dresden under professor Klengel. In 1804 he moved to Jelgava. He studied at the drawing school of Tartu University under Karl August Senff. From 1824 he worked as a drawing teacher at the Jelgava gymnasium.
