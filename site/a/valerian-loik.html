Valerian Loik

1904–1986 · Estonian painter (1904-1986)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

trained at Pallas

Loik is an Estonian artist who cultivated figure composition, nude, interior and view painting, as well as still life; portrait painting also occupies an important place in his oeuvre. He began with a narrative manner of treatment, but later became more generalising and forceful, adding intense colours to his work. Loik received his art education at N. Roth's private studio and at the Pallas art school as a student of Ado Vabbe. From 1950 to 1976 he worked as a teacher at ERKI. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
