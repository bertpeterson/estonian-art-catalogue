Anu Lehis

1993–
NOBA

Anu Lehis (b. 1993) is an artist who lives and works in Tallinn. She has completed the bachelor's programme in painting at the Estonian Academy of Arts (2017) and is currently studying in the master's programme in painting at the Estonian Academy of Arts. In addition, she has studied abroad at the Royal Academy of Art in The Hague (Koninklijke Academie van Beeldende Kunsten, Den Haag) in the fine arts speciality (2015-2016). Anu has participated in exhibitions since 2014. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
