Arne Maasik

1971– · Estonian architect and photographer (born 1971)
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Arne Maasik (b. 1971 in Tallinn) has worked as an architect and artist since 1995, when he graduated from the Estonian Academy of Arts with a bachelor's degree in architecture. In 1997 he completed his master's studies in the same department of architecture. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
