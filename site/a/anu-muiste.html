Anu Muiste


Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA; Vernissage

Anu Muiste (b. 1984) holds a master's degree in design from the Estonian Academy of Arts and a master's degree in social sciences from Tallinn University. She is an active member of the Estonian Painters' Association. Her background in psychology, design and the social sciences is also reflected in her work, in which she examines the relationships between the individual and society and the various levels of existence. Muiste's works are introspective, visually ambiguous and conceptually layered, inviting the viewer to perceive the world through organic forms and surrealism.
