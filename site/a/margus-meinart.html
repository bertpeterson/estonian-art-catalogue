Margus Meinart

1969– · Estonian painter (born 1969)
Artrovert; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at University of Tartu

Margus Meinart (b. 1969 in Tartu), after graduating from the Estonian Institute of Humanities in 1993, began studying furniture design and restoration at the Tartu Art School, graduating in this field in 1998. In 1994–1995 he studied painting in Norway, and in 1991–1996 in Tartu in Konrad Mägi's studio. Margus Meinart received the "Pallas" scholarship in 1998, as well as the Aino Järvesoo scholarship. He has been a member of the "Pallas" Art Society since 1994, and Meinart also belongs to the Tartu Artists' Union and the Tartu Restorers' Club.
