Ants Viidalepp

1921–2012 · Estonian painter and illustrator (1921-2012)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Hiiumaa Muuseumid; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vaal galerii

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

The artist was born in Tallinn, spent his childhood in Järvamaa. He has spent summers on Kassari, Hiiumaa, since the early 1960s, and since then has painted a great deal of the nature and people of Kassari. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
