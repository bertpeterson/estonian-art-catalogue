Romulus Tiitus

1906–1982 · Estonian caricaturist and writer (1906-1982)
Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Virumaa Muuseumid

trained at Pallas

He was elected chairman of the Estonian Writers' Union in 1939. In 1940–1941 he worked in the editorial office of the newspaper Kommunist and was editor-in-chief of the publishing house Ilukirjandus ja Kunst; he was also chairman of the organizing committee of the Estonian Soviet Writers' Union. During the Second World War he organized Estonian literary life in the rear of the USSR. In 1944–1946 and 1950–1954 he was chairman of the Estonian Soviet Writers' Union, and in 1950–1958 chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Council of the Estonian SSR and deputy chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme Council of the USSR.
