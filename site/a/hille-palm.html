Hille Palm

1941– · Estonian sculptor (born 1941)
E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Hille Palm studied from 1957 to 1962 at the Tartu Art School and afterwards at the Estonian State Art Institute, which she graduated from in 1972. Her work focuses on the expressive depiction of the female body and emotions. Palm has participated in international exhibitions and received several awards, including the Kristjan Raud Prize in 1985. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
