Pavel Kašutin


NOBA

Pavel Kašutin is a contemporary Estonian artist who works with stencil art, pop art and street art techniques. He was born and raised in Suurpea and currently lives and works in Loksa. He has been engaged in art since 2020, starting with expressionism and moving towards pop art. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
