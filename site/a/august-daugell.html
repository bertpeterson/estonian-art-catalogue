August Daugell

1830–1899 · Baltic German painter and printmaker (1830–1899)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Viljandi Muuseum

trained at Imperial Academy of Arts

One of the early Baltic wood engravers. Graduated from the Tartu district school, studied in F. L. von Maydell's wood engraving workshop in Tartu and as a visiting student at the Saint Petersburg Academy of Arts. Opened a wood engraving workshop on Vasilyevsky Island in Saint Petersburg, which supplied illustrations for newspapers, calendars, etc. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
