Kelli Valk

1978–
NOBA

After graduating from the Art Academy (1978), Kelli Valk has been active with exhibitions both in Estonia and abroad, holding solo exhibitions and participating in numerous joint projects in the art of printmaking. Her works, both in free graphic art and in book graphic art, have been noted with awards, and her works belong to the collections of several museums internationally.
