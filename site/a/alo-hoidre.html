Alo Hoidre

1916–1993
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Ajaloomuuseum; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii

The artist's family opted for Estonia in 1920. A. Hoidre studied graphic art at the State School of Applied Arts in 1933-1937, graduated from the State Higher Art School in 1941. In the same year he was mobilised into the Red Army, took part in battles and was in the artists' front brigade. From 1944-1982 he was a lecturer at ERKI (EKA).
