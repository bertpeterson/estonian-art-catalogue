Benita Vomm

1908–2005 · Estonian painter (1908-2005)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage; Viljandi Muuseum

trained at Pallas

1926-1929 studied at "Pallas". Received the qualification of drawing teacher (A. Vabbe's studio). In 1944 went to Germany, worked in 1945-48 as a drawing teacher at the Augsburg Estonian gymnasium. In 1948 moved to Venezuela, to Caracas. From 1953 lived in Toronto. Member of EKKT from 1956. Married to sculptor August Vomm. Children are artists Ants Erik Vomm and Mai Järve-Vomm. Until 1929 the surname was Mölder. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
