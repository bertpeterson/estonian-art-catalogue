Herman Talvik

1906–1984 · Estonian artist (1906-1984)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

From 1934 Herman Talvik began exhibiting at art exhibitions in Finland and Estonia; during World War II he worked as a teacher (mainly at Tallinn Secondary School), in 1944 he fled to Sweden, where he was a freelance artist. Solo exhibitions of this outstanding Estonian émigré artist were held in Estonia in 1971 and 1981 at the Art Museum of Estonia in Kadriorg Palace, in 1991 at the Tartu Art Museum, in 2006-2007 at the Art Museum of Estonia's Adamson-Eric Museum, and in 2016 at the Hiiumaa Museum.
