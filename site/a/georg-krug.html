Georg Krug

1908–1969 · - 1617
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Born in St. Petersburg as the son of a Swedish father and a Russian mother. In 1916–1917 studied at the Karl May Gymnasium in St. Petersburg. Came to Estonia in 1921, graduated from the Narva Russian Gymnasium. Studied drawing in Tallinn under K. Zeidler and E. Verber. In 1926–1928 studied graphic art at the State School of Applied Art, and in 1928–1930 studied painting at Pallas in Tartu (in the studios of N. Triik and A. Vabbe). Continued his training in Paris and worked in Estonia as a painter. He repeatedly visited the Pechory Monastery and at the age of 19 converted to Orthodoxy. After World War II he lived in Paris, entered monastic life and devoted himself to icon painting. He died at the Skete of the Holy Spirit near Mesnil-Saint-Denis in France.
