Georg Krug

1908–1969 · - 1617
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

He was born in St. Petersburg as the son of a Swedish father and a Russian mother. In 1916–1917 he studied at the Karl May Gymnasium in St. Petersburg. He came to Estonia in 1921 and finished the Narva Russian Gymnasium. He studied drawing in Tallinn under K. Zeidler and E. Verber. In 1926-1928 he studied graphic art at the State School of Applied Art, and in 1928-1930 painting at Pallas in Tartu (in the studios of N. Triik and A. Vabbe). He furthered his studies in Paris and worked in Estonia as a painter. He repeatedly visited the Pechory Monastery and, at the age of 19, converted to Orthodoxy. After the Second World War he lived in Paris, entered monastic life, and devoted himself to icon painting. He died at the Holy Spirit skete near Mesnil-Saint-Denis in France.
