Uno Roosvalt

1941– · Estonian painter (born 1941)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Haus Galerii; NOBA; Pärnu Muuseum

trained at Estonian Academy of Arts

Uno Roosvalt has received recognition throughout his creative career, including the diploma of the Moscow Youth Exhibition (1972, 1975), the bronze medal of the USSR Exhibition of Economic Achievements (1984), the Pühajärve Prize (1993 and 1994), the Prize of the Painters' Association (2000) and the Konrad Mägi Prize (2011).
