Jaan Luik

1953–
NOBA; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

Jaan Luik (b. 1953 in Tallinn) is an Estonian sculptor and art lecturer. Luik graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute (1979) and has also worked as a teacher at the Tartu Art School. He has been a member of the Estonian Artists' Union since 1990. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
