Juhan Raudsepp

1896–1984 · Estonian sculptor (1896-1984)
Dr. Fr. R. Kreutzwaldi Memoriaalmuuseum; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Pärnu Muuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum; Vernissage

Born on a farm in Lugaži (Luke) parish in Latvia, studied at Valga Town School and Penza Art School. Took part in the War of Independence. Worked as a drawing teacher 1920-25 in Tartu, 1925-29 in Tallinn. Was one of the founders of the Estonian Artists' Group in 1923. Made designs for War of Independence memorials, as a sculptor was a successful and talented autodidact. (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
