Rait Rosin

1981–
Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Haus Galerii; NOBA

Rait Rosin (b. 1981) earned a bachelor's degree in painting from the Estonian Academy of Arts (2005), a master's degree in art from the same institution (2007), and continues his studies in the doctoral programme in art and design at EKA (since 2015). Besides his artistic education, Rosin has earned a master's degree in philosophy from Tallinn University (2013). (machine translation of the holder's Estonian text, unedited)
