Marju Bormeister

1945– · Estonian painter (born 1945)
Allee galerii; E-Kunstisalong; Eesti Kunsti Oksjonid; Eesti Kunstimuuseum; NOBA

Marju Bormeister (b. 1945 in Tallinn) graduated from the Estonian State Art Institute as a painter in 1985. As a descendant of artists, she has lived half her life in the Tallinn Art Hall, encountering early on the turpentine-scented world of paints. Bormeister especially likes to paint nature and the various surface structures found in it. As a technique, Bormeister particularly enjoys monotype.
