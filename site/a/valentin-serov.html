Valentin Serov

1865–1911 · Russian painter (1865–1911)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

movement Impressionism · trained at Imperial Academy of Arts

Valentin Serov was born into the family of the composer Alexander Serov. In 1878–1879 he studied painting under the guidance of Ilya Repin, in 1880–1885 he studied at the Petersburg Academy of Arts. In 1897–1909 he worked as a pedagogue at the Moscow School of Painting, Sculpture and Architecture. He was one of the Peredvizhniki (from 1894, of the group).
