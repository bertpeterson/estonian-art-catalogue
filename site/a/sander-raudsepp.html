Sander Raudsepp

1988–
NOBA; Vaal galerii

Sander Raudsepp has studied ceramics at the Estonian Academy of Arts. In his work he draws inspiration mainly from conspiracy theories, religion, psychedelia and ambiguous jokes. Raudsepp encourages viewers to create connections between things that at first glance seem unrelated, and to think about topics that they would otherwise rather avoid.
