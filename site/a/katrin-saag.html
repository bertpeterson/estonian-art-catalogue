Katrin Saag

1974–
Haus Galerii; NOBA

Born in 1974 in Tallinn, Katrin studied fine arts at the Estonian Academy of Arts from 2005–2012. Most important recent exhibitions: "MIS VÄRVI ON ARMASTUS", Kunstiruum Gallery, Tallinn, Estonia (2021), MERRILY, MERRILY, MERRILY, Sakala Centre, Viljandi; Kõnnu Viinaköögi Gallery, Estonia (2021), IN THE LIGHT, Fruttis Gallery, Brazil, Abadiania (2021), 20th Spring Exhibition of Estonian Artists Association "Kunstnikud maalivad kunstnikke", Tallinn Art Hall, Estonia (2020), GAIA, Sargvere Manor, Estonia 2020, TROPICAL PASSION, Caminho Encantado Gallery, Brazil, Abadiania and in Kõnnu Viinaköögi Gallery, Estonia.
