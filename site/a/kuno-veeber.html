Kuno Veeber

1898–1929 · Estonian painter (1898-1929)
Eesti Kunstimuuseum; Eesti Teatri- ja Muusikamuuseum; Hiiumaa Muuseumid; Tartu Kunstimuuseum

trained at Gustav Adolf Grammar School · trained at Tartu Art School

Kuno Veeber was born as the second of six sons of the Adila manor manager. He studied medicine at the University of Tartu and served as a medical orderly in the Estonian War of Independence. His art studies began in Ants Laikmaa's studio, and continued with Nikolai Triik and Jaan Koort. In 1922-1924 Veeber studied at the art school Pallas and graduated in its first class in 1924. In 1923 he stayed in Germany, in Dresden, living with the artist Magnus Zeller. In 1924-1926 he continued his studies in Paris with André Lhote, serving as the senior member of his studio. It was in Paris that Kuno Veeber's style as an artist took shape. In 1926 he married Agathe Kanto (Veeber). In 1927 Veeber spent half a year in Italy on a Cultural Endowment scholarship, copying, for example, Tintoretto in the Doge's Palace in Venice. Kuno Veeber took his own life on 1 January 1929, and is buried at Rahumäe Cemetery in Tallinn. The gravestone monument, made by Jaan Koort, has been vandalised.
