Oskar RAUNAM (1914, Paide – 1992, Tallinn) was an Estonian painter and pedagogue who created realistic landscape, flower and portrait paintings as well as figural compositions; he also published several textbooks, e.g. "Akvarellimaal" (1952) and "Joonistamise ja maalimise õpik" (1961, 1963). Raunam obtained his art education in the decoration painting speciality at the State School of Applied Arts, in the drawing and draughting teacher speciality at the Tallinn Pedagogium, and in the painting speciality under the guidance of Johannes Greenberg at the State Higher Art School. He worked at ERKI as a teacher of drawing and painting, as vice-rector for study and research, and as head of the general painting department. /text by Liisi Aibel, 2013/.
