Robert Suvi

1947–
NOBA

Robert Suvi (born 8 June 1947 in Tartu) is an Estonian artist and educator, known primarily for his practice of monumental painting. He received his initial art education at the Tartu Art School from 1962 to 1967, studying in the speciality of artist-teacher. Later he continued his studies at the Estonian State Art Institute (ERKI), where he studied painting from 1971 to 1976, specialising in monumental painting.
